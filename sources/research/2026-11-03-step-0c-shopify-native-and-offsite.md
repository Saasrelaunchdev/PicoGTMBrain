## 3. The question the prompt asks: what does the free native option already do, and what does it not do

**What a Shopify merchant gets free, today, for the job Pico is bought for.**

1. **A storefront AI agent.** The Inbox agent answers product, policy, and order questions from the catalog, inventory, and policies, suggests products, can start checkout from the chat, applies discount codes at checkout, hands off to staff, and replies in the customer's language. With Shop sign-in it personalizes from purchase history across Shopify, which no third-party app can match.
2. **Meaning-based search** for eligible stores, now in more languages than English, with synonyms, filters, boosts, and recommendations.
3. **Unmet-demand reporting for the search bar.** "Searches with no results" and "Searches with no clicks" reports in Search & Discovery.
4. **Off-store discovery.** Products discoverable in ChatGPT, Copilot, AI Mode, Gemini, and Meta, for stores selling to the eligible buyer countries, plus a knowledge base the merchant publishes to those agents.
5. **Agent-readiness.** WebMCP tools on every Liquid and Hydrogen storefront, so browser agents can search, fill a cart, and reach checkout.

**What it does not do, or does not document, as of 2026-11-03.** Each item is "not found in Shopify's documentation", not "proven absent".

1. **Legacy customer accounts are excluded.** The Inbox agent requires new customer accounts. Stores still on legacy accounts cannot turn it on.
2. **Order lookups and personalization need sign-in,** and Shopify's own help warns that requiring sign-in adds friction. Sign-in is off by default.
3. **Arabic and right to left in the chat widget are not documented.** The agent replies in the customer's language, but the Inbox listing's interface languages do not include Arabic, and right to left rendering of the Inbox widget was not found. This is the one place where Pico's stated features (Arabic right to left, a language switcher, separate merchant copy per language) are not matched by anything found in Shopify's documentation. It must stay phrased as what Pico does, never as what Inbox lacks.
4. **Voice controls are limited to tone and human handoff,** per Shopify's description. Personality presets, warmth, length, emoji, clarification style, and tone guidelines of the kind Pico offers were not found.
5. **Conversational discovery analytics were not found.** Shopify reports zero-result and no-click terms for the search bar. A report of what shoppers asked the Inbox agent that the catalog could not answer was not found.
6. **Shopify only.** Nothing here serves Shopware 6 or custom stores.

**What this means for the brief.**

- **Slot 2 holds, and it is stronger than the brief says.** Inbox now starts checkout from chat and personalizes with Shop sign-in. Every Pico argument still has to survive "why not just turn on the Inbox agent". The honest openings are the five gaps above, not a quality claim.
- **Slot 3 holds, with two fact changes.** Semantic search is no longer English only. Shopify already shows zero-result and no-click search terms for free. **The second demand-driver candidate in `SKILL-ADAPTATION.md` Phase 1 fails its second rule as written:** a zero result report reveals a problem the merchant can already see in Shopify's own reports for the search bar. It survives only narrowed to conversational requests, phrases shoppers typed into the assistant, which Shopify's report does not cover. Phase 1 should score it that way.
- **Slot 6 holds and widens.** Agentic Storefronts is now one of several Shopify moves that put discovery inside agents: UCP catalog MCP servers, WebMCP on every storefront, Shop.md, and the agentic knowledge base. Pico's on-store job has to sit beside all of them, never against them.
- **New watchlist candidates from the platforms:** Microsoft Brand Agents (through Clarity) and Google's Business Agent. Both are brand agents offered by platforms the merchant already uses.
- **Pico's build path is Shopify's.** The Storefront Catalog MCP, the per-store UCP endpoint, and WebMCP are the same rails a developer would use to build Pico's job from scratch. That is the build-it slot, profiled in 0.D.
