**What this means for Pico.** Two facts reach beyond Run 2. First, Shopify's revenue share on Pico's App Store income is 0% up to $1M lifetime, then 15%, which Run 1 Phase 9 needs for net price. Second, **candidate C11 (free installs on development stores) is buildable with Shopify's own billing**: mark Growth or Pro as free for testing. That turns C11 from a program cost into a setting, which Run 2 Phase 1 should score accordingly. And Shopify itself now pays agencies a GMV-linked share for four years, which raises what an agency expects from any app program.

## 3. Summary: what a Shopify agency expects, at minimum, and what the best programs add

### The floor, present in nearly every program found

1. **A recurring revenue share around 20%, for 12 to 36 months.** The observed range runs from 5% (Rebuy managed share) to 50% (one Zipchat page). Most cluster at 20%.
2. **A tier ladder with a name the agency can display.** Partner, Premier, Elite, or Registered, Gold, Platinum. 0.F found 11 of 26 agency pages displaying vendor tiers.
3. **A partner portal** to register leads and see referrals and payouts, usually on PartnerStack or a vendor portal.
4. **Training and certification.**
5. **A listing in the vendor's agency directory,** which sends the agency work (Gorgias, Rebuy, Klaviyo, Tidio all run one).

### What the best programs add

| Addition | Who offers it |
|---|---|
| Lifetime revenue share | Rep AI Elite (25%), Octane AI (20%), Tidio affiliate (30%), Zipchat agency (50% on one page) |
| One-time bounty on top | Gorgias Elite (15%), Tidio agency (up to $27,000 per referral) |
| **Pay for managing, not only for referring** | Rebuy's 5% managed share, paid regardless of who referred the brand |
| Client discount the agency can pass on | Rep AI Elite (15%), LoyaltyLion (10% split option) |
| **Resale under the agency's brand at the agency's price** | Zipchat |
| Dedicated partner manager, event budget, co-marketing | Gorgias Premier and Elite, Octane |
| Billable implementation work | Recharge (revenue share plus billable hours) |
| A multi-store view | Klaviyo client health dashboards, Intercom Fin multi-store workspaces, and Shopify's own Partner Dashboard for store access |

### Where Pico stands against this, per the source

No program, no tier, no directory, no partner portal, no multi-store plan (each plan connects one store), no dev store policy stated. What exists: the sandbox, the setup skill, and roles. **The precondition result `../SKILL-ADAPTATION.md` predicted for Run 2 is confirmed by the benchmarks:** a multi-store or agency workspace (C3) and a recurring share (C10) are the floor, not differentiators. C11 is now cheap to provide through Shopify App Pricing. The numbers were set later on 2026-11-03 in `../DECISIONS.md` D22, D23, and D33 to D41.
