# Run 2: Rules for the owner's chosen channels

**Date checked:** 2026-11-03. **Why this file exists:** the owner chose email, LinkedIn, Upwork, and Fiverr for Run 2 on 2026-11-03, and each channel has rules that shape the funnel, the partnership offer, and the assets. **Feeds:** `../run-2-implementers/07-customer-journey-funnel/`, `08-assets-and-collaterals/`, `09-pricing-and-packaging/`.

**Method and limits.** Web search only, because this session's network policy blocks page loads. The pages are the platforms' own terms and help centers, except where marked. **This is research, not legal advice.** The partnership program needs a legal check of each platform's current terms before launch.

---

## 1. Upwork

| Rule | What it says | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **24-month non-circumvention** | For 24 months from the start of a relationship found on Upwork, payments with that person must stay on Upwork, unless the client pays a Conversion Fee to take the relationship off the platform. A breach is material and can lead to permanent suspension | [Upwork: circumvention](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360052511133-Circumvention-and-why-it-s-against-the-rules), [Upwork Legal](https://www.upwork.com/legal) |
| Conversion Fee | An undated page gives $1,000 to $50,000 per relationship. Unverified for 2026. After conversion, Upwork no longer supports the relationship | [Upwork: Conversion Fee](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043723533-What-is-the-Upwork-Conversion-Fee) |
| Client marketplace fee | 5% on Basic, 10% on Business Plus, per a pricing page about 855 days old. Stale | [Upwork pricing](https://www.upwork.com/pricing/client) |

**What it means for the plan.** The paid usability engagement fits Upwork well: post a fixed-price job and pay through Upwork. **The partnership program does not automatically follow.** Paying affiliate incentives off Upwork to a freelancer met on Upwork, inside 24 months, may count as circumvention. Options to check with counsel: keep partner payouts on Upwork, pay the Conversion Fee, or keep the Upwork relationship to the engagement only and recruit partners through Pico's own channels.

## 2. Fiverr

| Rule | What it says | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Payments only through Fiverr** | Users may not offer or accept payments by any method other than placing an order on Fiverr | [Fiverr Terms of Service](https://www.fiverr.com/legal-portal/legal-terms/terms-of-service) |
| **No steering off Fiverr** | The Community Standards bar proposing to bypass Fiverr's payment system, directing clients or freelancers to external platforms, and sharing contact details for off-platform business. Warnings, then restriction, then suspension | [Community Standards](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/32242973123985-Our-Community-Standards), [Off-platform policy](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/12792122691601-Stay-protected-Fiverr-s-off-platform-policy), [Enforcement](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/22569899568913-How-we-enforce-policies) |
| How a buyer starts | Order an existing gig, or post a brief and receive offers | [How Fiverr works](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010558038-How-Fiverr-works) |
| Buyer service fee | 5.5% of the purchase, plus $3.50 on orders under $200, per the help center. Older schedules conflict | [Fiverr help](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050216133-Paying-for-orders-extras-or-custom-offers) |

**What it means for the plan.** Fiverr is the strictest of the four. The engagement must be ordered and paid on Fiverr. **Inviting a Fiverr freelancer, inside Fiverr, to join an external partner program, or asking for their email, can breach the Community Standards.** Keep Fiverr to paid usability work. Recruit partners through email, LinkedIn, and Pico's own pages, where an implementer can find and join independently.

## 3. Cold email (CAN-SPAM, United States)

| Requirement | What the FTC says | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Covers commercial email, with **no exception for business-to-business email** | [FTC CAN-SPAM guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) |
| Headers and subject | Accurate From, To, Reply-To, and routing. A subject line that matches the content | Same |
| Identification | The message identifies itself as an advertisement, with leeway in how | Same |
| Address | A valid physical postal address: street, USPS box, or registered private mailbox | Same |
| Opt-out | A working opt-out, honored within 10 business days, with no fee and no extra information required | Same |
| Consent | No opt-in required before sending | Same |
| Outside the US | Researched later on 2026-11-03 in `2026-11-03-tbd-resolution-research.md` Section 4. UK PECR allows B2B email to companies and LLPs with an opt-out, and treats sole traders as individuals. Germany requires prior consent. Decision: US recipients only in the first 90 days (`../DECISIONS.md` D48) | [ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/) |

**What it means for the plan.** The skill's default Phase 8 channel, "LinkedIn Outreach (HeyReach)", names an automation tool. For Pico, LinkedIn outreach is planned as manual: a person sends each connection request and message. Volume follows from that.

## 5. Benchmarks for the paid engagement fee

Vendor-published research incentive guides, for structure. Pico's fee was set from them later on 2026-11-03: $150 for a session of up to 90 minutes, $75 for a 30-minute interview (`../DECISIONS.md` D33, D34).

| Benchmark | Figure | Source | Mark |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineers, marketers, designers, 60-minute session | $100 to $150 | [Great Question](https://greatquestion.co/blog/customer-research-incentives-guide) | `VENDOR` |
| B2B individual contributors | $75 to $150 an hour. Directors and senior managers $150 to $300 | [CleverX](https://cleverx.com/blog/how-to-incentivize-b2b-research-participants-rates-formats-and-best-practices/) | `VENDOR` |
| Overall midpoint | About $145 an hour, from a User Interviews 2025 report as cited by Koji | [Koji](https://www.koji.so/blog/respondent-pricing-2026) | `SECONDHAND` |
| No-shows by incentive | 10% at $60 an hour against 6% at $100, as cited | Same | `SECONDHAND` |
| Freelance AI chat setup gigs | About $100 to install and configure an existing AI chat app on a Shopify store | `2026-11-03-step-0d-build-hire-nothing.md`, F07 | Marketplace listing |

**Reading it.** A usability session is closer to a paid gig than to a research interview. The freelance gig price (about $100 for an install) and the research incentive range ($100 to $150 an hour) frame the same order of magnitude. Platform fees (Upwork, Fiverr) sit on top.
