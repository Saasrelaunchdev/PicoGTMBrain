## 3. What Step 1.1 means for Phase 1

1. **Table stakes dominate the stated feature set.** Rows 1, 2, 3, 10, 13, 16, and 21 are wanted by most or all, and most vendors offer them.
2. **Pico's differentiators sit where US merchant demand is low:** the sandbox (19), the setup skill (20), Shopware (17), the custom API (18), and local payment (22). Row 7's right to left combination matters to the GCC, the later market.
3. **One stated row could reach the Hero quadrant: predictable cost (14).** Demand is high because billing surprise is the commonest complaint, and few vendors make cost visible and capped. It was conditional on a conversation being defined and the behavior at quota being decided (open decision 7). Both were decided later on 2026-11-03 (`../DECISIONS.md` D24, D25), so it holds.
4. **The roadmap rows with the most weight are table stakes Pico lacks:** revenue attribution (C1), handoff (C5), order answers (C6), and integrations (C8). Holdout measurement (C9) is the one candidate that could differentiate if built, and it would answer the category's lift claims.
