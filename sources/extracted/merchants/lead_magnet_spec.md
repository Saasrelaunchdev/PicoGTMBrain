# Run 1, Lead Magnet: Shopify Store Findability Scorecard

The skill's Lead Magnet Specification for ScoreApp, built on the Phase 1 demand driver, the **Shopper Words Test** (Dashboard 1, Step 1.6), and its fifth variant, the Store Findability Scorecard. USA first. Built on 2026-11-03. Implementation copy is in `scorecard_spec.docx`.

Rules applied: the score is the merchant's self-assessment, not a measurement of their store, and every result message says so. No industry statistic is quoted, because Step 0.H found them all secondhand. No message says the store is losing sales. Shopify's own reports are referenced as useful, never as lacking. The day 2 email uses a labelled sandbox example until a case study clears the intake rule.

## Sheet: Overview

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| Scorecard Name | Shopify Store Findability Scorecard |
| Target ICP | US Shopify merchants in help-me-choose categories, ICP Levels 1 and 2 (Dashboard 4): owners on Level 1, ecommerce managers on Level 2 |
| Problem it Reveals | Whether shoppers who describe a product in their own words are likely to find it, judged from catalog size and likeness, the words in product titles, how pre-purchase questions arrive, and whether the merchant can see what shoppers asked for |
| Immediate Value to User | A score from 0 to 100, a tier, and three fixes for that tier, plus the Shopper Words Test worksheet to check ten real phrases against their own catalog |
| Conversion Path to Pico | Scorecard, then results page with three fixes, then the Shopper Words Test worksheet, then the sandbox store, then install from the App Store on the free plan. High scores are invited to the founding cohort (Dashboard 5) |

## Sheet: Questions

Higher score means more findability risk, and a stronger fit for Pico. Points per question add to a maximum of 100.

| # | Question | Answer Options (4-5 options) | Scoring Logic | What It Reveals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How many products does your store sell? | Fewer than 25. 25 to 99. 100 to 499. 500 to 1,999. 2,000 or more | 0, 4, 8, 12, 15 | Catalog size. Very small catalogs rarely need conversational discovery |
| 2 | How alike are your products? | Mostly one of a kind. Some are similar. Many differ only by specs, size, or fit. Many need a compatibility or fitment check | 0, 5, 10, 15 | Choice complexity, the core of Pico's niche |
| 3 | When shoppers ask before buying, what do they ask most? | We rarely get questions. Shipping and returns. Which product fits my need. Whether it is compatible, the right size, or right for a use. I don't know | 0, 3, 10, 15, 8 | Whether pre-purchase questions are about choosing, which Pico handles, or about orders, which Pico does not |
| 4 | Have you looked at "Searches with no results" in Shopify's search analytics? | Yes, monthly, and we act on it. Yes, now and then. Once or twice. Never. I didn't know it existed | 0, 5, 10, 15, 15 | Whether the merchant already reads the signal Shopify gives them, and how much is unexplored |
| 5 | Do your product titles and descriptions use the words shoppers use? | Yes, we check them against customer language. Mostly, we write for search engines. Mostly manufacturer or technical names. Not sure | 0, 5, 12, 15 | The words gap the Shopper Words Test checks |
| 6 | How do shoppers who don't know a product's name find it today? | Browsing collections and filters works well. They use the search bar. They email or chat and we answer by hand. Many seem to leave. Not sure | 3, 6, 12, 15, 10 | How much discovery depends on knowing the name, and how much falls on the team |
| 7 | Can you see what shoppers asked for, in their own words, before they bought or left? | Yes, in one report. Partly, from search terms. Only from emails and chats we read by hand. No | 0, 5, 8, 10 | Insight into requests, vector 3 in Dashboard 2 |
