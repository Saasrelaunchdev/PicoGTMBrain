# Run 1, Step 2.16: Positioning summary, Shopify merchants

Internal analysis, not customer-facing copy. Built from `competitive_analysis.xlsx` (vectors in Vectors_2.8) on 2026-11-03.

## How Pico can lead the category

Pico can lead on the two vectors where the slate scores lowest: **insight into what shoppers ask**, and **answers traceable to the catalog**. Pico's analytics show the discovery funnel from request to result, click, and cart, with the phrases that returned nothing or drew few clicks, and every product card comes from the synced catalog with a sync log behind it. That changes the first conversation with a merchant from "trust our lift number" to "see what your shoppers asked for", which the merchant can check in the sandbox and then on their own store on the free plan. It answers the complaints reviewers repeat most often after billing surprises: answers they cannot check, and results they never see. The room to grow is large and sourced only at the top: 1,253,193 live US Shopify stores (StoreLeads, 2026). No tracker measures the share whose shoppers ask in words the catalog does not use, so the working frame is about 54,300 US stores in the five help-me-choose categories with 100 or more products (`DERIVED`, Dashboard 4). Every install is a choice against a free, native agent on about 358,000 stores, so Pico has to win on what the merchant learns, not on what the assistant can do.

## Defensibility

The lead is narrow and can be copied. Shopify already reports search-bar terms with no results and no clicks, and Tidio lists questions Lyro could not answer, so a conversational report is one release away for either. The defensible part is what follows the report: a loop that turns each zero-result phrase into a catalog fix and shows whether the fix worked (holdout measurement, candidate C9, and the proof capture point in `GTM-BRIEF.md` Section 5), plus the history of requests each store builds up with use. If Pico ships that loop first and implementers adopt it as their audit tool (Run 2), the lead compounds. If it stays a report, it is a feature, and it will be matched.
