# Run 1, Dashboard 5: Buying Committee, Shopify merchants

Steps 5.1 and 5.2 of gtm-builder v3.1 for the two ICP levels in Dashboard 4, USA first. Built on 2026-11-03. Following `SKILL-ADAPTATION.md` Phase 5: the committee collapses for Level 1 stores, holds for Level 2, and Level 2 gains a fourth column for the implementer who controls the theme. Shoppers are not on the committee. Their benefit is evidence for the merchant.

"To test" marks a channel with no evidence in this run's research. Everything else traces to `research/`.

## Sheet: ICP1_Personas

### The committee collapses: one person holds all three roles

On a Level 1 store the owner is the user, the manager, and the decision maker. The skill's three columns would repeat one person three times, so this sheet fills one column and moves the effort to the Design_Partners sheet.

| Attribute | Store owner: User, Manager, and Decision Maker |
|---|---|
| Possible Job Titles | Founder, owner, co-founder, store manager on a small team |
| Responsibility Area | Everything: catalog, theme, apps, marketing, customer questions, and the budget |
| Purchasing Influence | Sole decision. Installs, approves the charge in Shopify Billing, and cancels by uninstalling |
| Core Customer Journey Stages | All seven. Most time in stage 2 (App Store search, listings, reviews), stage 4 (install and the activation checklist), and stage 7 (keeps paying or uninstalls, every month) |
| Potential Reasons to Block | Fear of a bill that grows without warning. Setup time. Worry that the assistant tells shoppers something wrong. Doubt that it does more than the free Inbox agent. Risk to the theme. The free quota running out mid-month. No reviews yet |
| Drivers | Fewer repeat questions answered by hand. Shoppers finding products without the owner's help. Seeing what shoppers ask for. A start with no card and no commitment |
| Assets to Get Buy-In | The sandbox store. An App Store listing with screenshots of product cards and the Analytics page. The four-step setup checklist. A plan page that states each quota. The Shopper Words Test (Dashboard 1 demand driver). A short walkthrough video |
| Channels to Find Them | Shopify App Store search, categories, and the "AI Shopping Assistants" collection. Google and AI answer engines for category searches. Vendor-written roundups. Shopify Community forums. Their agency or freelancer. YouTube tutorials (to test) |

## Sheet: Design_Partners

### Step 5.2: the founding cohort of merchants

Pico is pre-launch, so this profile defines the first merchants, the founding cohort. It is also where the proof capture rules in `GTM-BRIEF.md` Section 5 attach to real accounts.

| Attribute | Specification |
|---|---|
| Design Partner Attributes | A US Shopify store in one of the five help-me-choose categories, with 100 or more products, so that browsing fails (D53), on Basic, Grow, or Advanced, run by someone who opens their own analytics, willing to install an app with no reviews yet and to give feedback |
| Job Titles | Founder or owner on Level 1 stores. Ecommerce manager or head of ecommerce on Level 2 stores |
| Demographics | US-based. A store team of 1 to 10 people. Annual online sales of about $150,000 to $3 million, a planning band (D53). 10,000 to 100,000 monthly sessions. English storefront, a second language welcome |
| Responsibility Areas | Catalog, apps, customer questions, merchandising |
| Current Tools | Shopify Search & Discovery. Often Shopify Inbox, with or without the agent turned on. On Level 2, a search app (Boost, Searchanise, Smart Product Filter & Search) or a quiz app. Google Analytics |
| Key Pain Points | The same pre-purchase questions, again and again. Shoppers who cannot find a product they sell. No view of what shoppers ask an assistant |
| Critical Triggers | A new product line. Preparing for peak season. Switching chat apps. Opening "Searches with no results" and wanting to know what shoppers meant |
| Goals & Motivations | Shape a tool built for their category. Hands-on onboarding and a direct line to the team. Learn what their shoppers ask for |
| Decision Criteria | Setup time. Whether they can check the answers, since cards come from the synced catalog. Cost they can predict. How fast the team replies |
| Influence Level | The owner signs alone on Level 1. The manager recommends and the founder signs on Level 2 |
| Barriers to Purchase | No reviews yet. Fear of billing surprises. Theme risk. Sharing catalog and conversation data |
| Information Sources | Shopify App Store, Shopify Community, their agency or freelancer, AI answer engines |
| Success Metrics | Only what Pico measures: conversations, product click-through rate, add to cart rate, zero result rate on requests, and the number of catalog fixes made from the report. Targets for each founding store: the widget seen within 24 hours of install, a first conversation within 7 days, and 3 catalog fixes made from the report in 60 days. The zero result rate on requests in days 31 to 60 is compared with days 1 to 30 as the store's own baseline (D44). No sales figure is a success metric until revenue attribution exists |
| Early Adopter Characteristics | Tries new apps, answers email, will join a monthly 30-minute feedback call (D46), and is comfortable being told the product is early |

### How the cohort runs

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| Cohort size | 20 US stores (D30) |
| Where they come from | The Shopper Words Test and the Findability Scorecard (Dashboard 1 and the lead magnet). The App Store listing. Client stores brought by Run 2 implementers |
| What they get | Hands-on onboarding. A direct line to the founder. Input into the roadmap. 50% off any paid plan for the first 6 billing periods, through a Shopify Billing subscription discount (D30) |
| What they give | Feedback on setup and the Analytics page. Permission to cite outcomes, only if they agree, recorded under the intake rule |
| Proof capture | 1. The permission clause in the terms of service. 2. The capture point at value realization, for example when a store's zero result rate on requests first falls after a catalog fix. 3. A row in the proof set only when the point, its exact wording, its source, and recorded permission can all be filled |
| Exit | When 20 stores have joined or 90 days after the listing goes live, whichever comes first (D30). The cohort opens on 2026-12-01, after Cyber Monday. A written case study request goes to each store at day 60. After the exit, the App Store listing carries the self-serve motion on its own. If it fills before day 60, a second cohort of 20 opens |
