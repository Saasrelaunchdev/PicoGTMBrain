## Sheet: QA_Rules

Applied to every asset in the Library sheet before it is published.

| # | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Claims use the narrowed wording in GTM-BRIEF Section 5. Nothing says sales, revenue, conversion, or ROI about Pico | GTM-BRIEF Section 5 |
| 2 | No testimonial, review count, install count, or named merchant until the proof set has a row with recorded permission | Proof set, intake rule |
| 3 | No comparative claim about a named competitor without a sourced, dated Phase 2 finding, rechecked on the competitor's own page | User rules, GTM-BRIEF Section 5 |
| 4 | Never disparage Shopify or its tools. Describe what Pico does | GTM-BRIEF Section 5 |
| 5 | No em dashes, and none of the seven words on the "Also avoid" list at the end of GTM-BRIEF Section 5 | User rules, GTM-BRIEF Section 5 |
| 6 | Sample data is labelled as sample data | This dashboard |
| 7 | GCC and Arabic-market copy stays out of US-first assets | Owner decision, 2026-11-03 |
