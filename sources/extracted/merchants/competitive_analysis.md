### Why each vector

| Vector | Source of the vector | What a high score needs |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Easy setup with responsive help | The top two praise themes across reviews: responsive vendor support and quick setup | Installs in minutes, and a person answers when it breaks |
| 2. Support automation breadth | Reviewers praise agents that resolve most questions and cut tickets | Order status, returns, policies, handoff, and channels |
| 3. Insight into what shoppers ask | Reversed from "results not arriving": Inbox's report cannot pinpoint a conversation, Tidio's reporting called inaccurate. Dialog is praised for the opposite | The merchant sees what shoppers asked, how requests move from result to click to cart, and which phrases found nothing |
| 4. Answers traceable to the catalog | Reversed from "made-up answers": an Inbox reviewer asked for source visibility, Tidio and Gorgias reviewers report wrong answers | Product cards drawn from the synced catalog, and a sync log that shows when the data was last updated |
