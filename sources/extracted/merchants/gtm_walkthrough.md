# SLIDE 1, The Pico GTM dashboard suite for Shopify merchants

- Pico is an AI shopping assistant that lets shoppers describe what they want in plain language and returns products from the merchant's own synced catalog
- Category: AI shopping assistant, sold through the Shopify App Store, between chat and search and discovery
- Core claim: Pico shows merchants what their shoppers ask for, and answers shoppers with products drawn from the store's synced catalog
- Primary market: Shopify merchants, global, USA first. The GCC is a secondary market for later
- Evidence state: pre-launch. No installs, reviews, or results yet, so every claim is one a merchant can check now (D4)
- So what: every channel, message, and price in this plan traces back to one of nine dashboards

---

# SLIDE 2, Product Feature Matrix Dashboard, what we learned and what we lead with

- 22 stated features and 12 candidates, scored on demand and competitiveness against a 22-name watchlist
- Table stakes: natural language requests, product cards, add to cart, voice controls, the free plan
- Differentiators sit where US demand is low today: the sandbox store, the coding agent setup skill, discovery analytics on what shoppers asked the assistant
- Missing table stakes for the roadmap: revenue attribution, human handoff, order and policy answers, marketing integrations
- Demand driver: the Shopper Words Test, which shows the merchant requests their catalog could not answer
- So what: we lead acquisition with something a merchant can check on their own store, not with a lift number

# SLIDE 3, Competitive Market Analysis Dashboard, competitor map and buying context

- Slate: Chatty, Shopify Inbox with the Inbox agent, Shopify Search & Discovery, Tidio with Lyro, build or hire, and Shopify's agentic commerce stack
- The free native agent is the default comparison, on about 358,000 stores (StoreLeads, 2026)
- Reviewers praise responsive support and easy setup most
- Reviewers complain most about billing surprises, then setup and theme conflicts, slow support, made-up answers, and results they never see
- Shopify Search & Discovery already reports search-bar terms with no results, so Pico's insight is about what shoppers ask an assistant
- So what: every install is a choice against a free tool, so Pico wins on what the merchant learns, never by running down the platform

# SLIDE 5, ICP Dashboard, who we win first, and why

- Level 1: US Shopify stores with many similar or technical products, on Shopify's own search, no AI shopping assistant yet
- Level 2: stores already paying for a search, quiz, or chat app whose shoppers still miss products
- Categories: health, outdoor and sports, pets, auto parts, consumer electronics, up to about 192,500 US stores before overlap (StoreLeads, 2026)
- Bands: 100 or more products, 10,000 to 100,000 monthly sessions, about $150,000 to $3 million a year. About 54,300 US stores after the catalog cutoff (DERIVED)
- GCC: at least about 38,400 Shopify stores across six countries, shown for later and opened by a stated trigger (D11)
- So what: Level 1 is reached through the App Store, Level 2 through install trackers and the implementers who run their stores

# SLIDE 6, Buying Committee Dashboard, personas, blockers, drivers

- Level 1 collapses into one person: the owner installs, decides, and cancels
- Level 2 holds: ecommerce manager as user, head of ecommerce as manager, founder as decision maker
- A fourth column for the implementer, who rarely signs and can block by never enabling the theme embed
- Blockers: billing surprises, theme risk, no reviews yet, no revenue attribution
- Drivers: a list of catalog fixes, a start with no card, a predictable plan price
- So what: the sandbox and the setup checklist do the persuading that a sales team would do elsewhere

# SLIDE 7, Customer Journey Funnel Dashboard, stage progression and internal KPIs

- Stage 2 happens on the App Store, in roundups, and in AI assistants
- Stage 4 is an activation sub-funnel: install, authorize, enable the theme embed, sync, confirm the widget was seen
- Theme embeds are off by default after install, so a store can install Pico and never use it
- KPIs: listing to install rate, time to widget seen, trial to paid rate, monthly uninstall rate, with targets: 3% listing to install, 65% widget seen within 24 hours, 15% trial to paid by day 30, under 15% monthly uninstalls (D44)
- Stage 7 is a monthly charge that ends with an uninstall, so the uninstall reason is the renewal conversation
- So what: activation, not awareness, decides whether an install becomes a paying store

# SLIDE 8, Lead Magnet, scorecard funnel, what it reveals and why it converts

- Shopify Store Findability Scorecard, seven questions, scored 0 to 100
- Reveals catalog likeness, the words gap in product titles, how questions arrive, and whether the merchant can see what shoppers asked
- Three tiers, from "Shoppers can find their way" to "Shoppers ask in words your catalog doesn't use"
- Path: results with three fixes, the Shopper Words Test worksheet, the sandbox store, a free install
- Day 2 uses a labelled sandbox example. A case study replaces it only after it clears the intake rule
- So what: the merchant reaches the sandbox already knowing what to look for

# SLIDE 9, Assets and Collaterals Library Dashboard, channel coverage and TOFU to BOFU flow

- Three channels: the Shopify App Store, content and search including AI answer engines, and the product-led lifecycle
- The Shopper Words Test is the top-of-funnel asset in every channel: listing screenshots, a worksheet guide, a guided sandbox tour
- Middle: pricing section and demo store, comparison pages with sourced and dated facts, the activation checklist and first insight email
- Bottom: review requests after value realization, the breakeven worksheet, the trial to paid sequence
- Held back: paid ads and cold outbound to store owners until the listing converts
- So what: each asset hands the merchant to the next stage without a salesperson

# SLIDE 10, Pricing and Packaging Dashboard, value metric, usage metric, tiers, expansion levers

- Value metric: per conversation, which holds. One conversation is a thread with at least one AI reply, once per shopper and device per 24 hours (D24)
- Usage metric: stores per plan, one store per plan today
- Tiers: Free, 50 conversations a month. Growth, 600 for $49. Pro, 2,000 for $99. Scale, 6,000 for $299. Yearly plans give two months free
- Scale fills the empty anchor slot (D27). Agencies get a free workspace instead of a price, because Shopify Billing charges each store (D41)
- Expansion levers: conversation quota, stores, billing period
- So what: a quota the merchant can see, with alerts and a pause instead of overage, answers the category's top complaint (D25)
