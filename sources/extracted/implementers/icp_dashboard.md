# Run 2, Dashboard 4: Ideal Customer Profile, Shopify implementers

Step 4.1 of gtm-builder v3.1, with the maturity reading from `SKILL-ADAPTATION.md` Phase 4: Level 1 has never put an AI shopping assistant in its standard build, Level 2 has deployed AI chat for clients before and wants something repeatable it can maintain across stores. Implementers who serve US stores come first, wherever they are based. Gulf agencies are on their own sheet, for later. Built on 2026-11-03.

Rules applied: implementers are described as segments and counts, never as named prospects. Each count keeps its own source, method, and date, and counts from different sources are never added together. Field changes from the adaptation file: "Revenue, $" reads as the implementer's revenue band, "Business Model" is how they charge clients, and three rows are added: Partner Directory tier, number of active client stores, and services offered. Bands no source publishes are planning bands from `DECISIONS.md` D55, asked in the session application form.
