# Run 2, Dashboard 6: Value Proposition, Shopify implementers

Steps 6.1 and 6.2 of gtm-builder v3.1 for implementers, standalone. Built on 2026-11-03 from Dashboards 1 to 5.

**Rules applied in every table below.**

- The proof set is empty and closed for customer proof. In all nine copy drills, the template's Social Proof headline is written as an **Open Proof headline**, and the **Supporting Proof Point** names something the implementer can check now, and where (`DECISIONS.md` D4). Each drill keeps its **Method or process line**.
- The paid sessions start in week 1, the partner page goes live in week 6, and case studies follow the intake rule. Copy uses the narrowed Run 2 wording in `GTM-BRIEF.md` Section 5 and the decided terms: $150 a session, 25% of each referred store's subscription for 24 months, 30% for lead partners (`DECISIONS.md` D33, D35). No traffic volume is promised (D39). Nothing promises income, leads, certification, or white label.
- Nothing says sales, revenue, conversion, or ROI about Pico or about the implementer's clients. No competitor is named. Shopify's tools are never described.
- **Platform rule.** Copy about the partner program (VP 3, RTB 3) is used only on Pico's own pages, in email, and on LinkedIn, never inside Upwork or Fiverr, where steering a freelancer off the platform can breach the terms (`research/2026-11-03-run-2-channel-rules.md`).
- Body copy is 25 words or fewer, counted.
