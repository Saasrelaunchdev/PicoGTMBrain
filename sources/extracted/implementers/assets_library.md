# Run 2, Dashboard 8: Assets and Collaterals Library, Shopify implementers

Step 8.1 of gtm-builder v3.1, keeping the skill's three by three grid with the owner's three channels, decided 2026-11-03: **cold email to implementers, LinkedIn outreach, and job posts on Upwork and Fiverr** for the paid usability engagement. The partner program, developer distribution, and co-marketing are what these channels lead to, not channels of their own (`SKILL-ADAPTATION.md` Phase 8). The Phase 1 demand driver, the **Paid Usability Session**, is the top-of-funnel asset in each channel. Built on 2026-11-03.

Timeframes are production estimates for planning, not commitments, sized to the two-person planning basis in `DECISIONS.md` D6. They shrink if the team is larger. Contributors are roles. Every asset follows the QA_Rules and Platform_Rules sheets.

## Sheet: Platform_Rules

From `research/2026-11-03-run-2-channel-rules.md`, read through the web search index on 2026-11-03. **Research, not legal advice.** Counsel reviews each platform's current terms in week 5, before the partner page goes live (D50).

| Channel | Rule | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| Upwork | For 24 months from the start of a relationship found on Upwork, payments with that person stay on Upwork, unless a conversion fee is paid. A breach can lead to permanent suspension | The paid session fits Upwork. Partnership payouts to someone met on Upwork may not move off it within 24 months. Options: keep payouts on Upwork, pay the conversion fee, or recruit partners only through Pico's own channels |
| Fiverr | Payment only through a Fiverr order. No proposals to bypass Fiverr payment, no directing users to outside platforms, no sharing contact details for business off Fiverr | Fiverr is for paid usability work only. No partner invitation, no email request, no outside link meant to move the relationship |
| Cold email | CAN-SPAM covers business email: accurate headers and subject, identified as an advertisement, a postal address, an opt-out honored within 10 business days. No opt-in needed in the US | Every email carries the US mailbox address (D47) and a working opt-out. US recipients only in the first 90 days. Then UK companies and LLPs only, never UK sole traders or ordinary partnerships, and never cold to the EU (D48) |
| LinkedIn | The User Agreement bars bots and automated messaging, and third-party tools that scrape or automate | Outreach is by hand. The skill's default tool for this channel is an automation tool, so it is not used |
