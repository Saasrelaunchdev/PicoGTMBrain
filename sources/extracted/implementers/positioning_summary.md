# Run 2, Step 2.16: Positioning summary, Shopify implementers

Internal analysis, not customer-facing copy. Built from `competitive_analysis.xlsx` (vectors in Vectors_2.8) on 2026-11-03.

## How Pico can lead the category

Pico can lead on the two vectors where every program found is weakest: **paid from the first engagement**, and **proof the implementer co-owns**. Every program on the slate pays after a referral or a closed deal. The owner's path pays an implementer for a usability session on first contact, then builds a case study with their client store and credits them for it, which is the implementer's marketing as much as Pico's first proof. That turns cold email, LinkedIn messages, and Upwork and Fiverr job posts into offers of paid work rather than pitches, which fits how freelancers already sell: app installs for about $100, with research sessions for developers benchmarked at $100 to $150 an hour (vendor guides). The pool is large but only partly counted: in the US alone, 33 Platinum, 45 Premier, 255 Plus, and 342 Select Partner Directory agencies (March 2026, an agency-published guide, secondary), plus Fiverr's 40,000+ Shopify gig results and an Upwork pool it does not count, with 4,810 client reviews on its US Shopify developers page. Each implementer serves several client stores, 2 to 10 for a freelancer and 10 to 100 for an agency in the planning bands (D55), so one partner can mean repeat deployments.

## Defensibility

Paying for sessions is easy to copy, so the engagement alone is not a moat. What compounds is what it produces: case studies credited to implementers, owned jointly and gated by permission, and implementers who have put Pico on several client stores and have a reason to keep doing it, which needs a multi-store workspace, built once 10 partners each have 3 or more active stores (C3, D23). The gap between the first session and the partnership has to close fast, because Gorgias, Klaviyo, and the Shopify Partner Program set the floor implementers expect: a recurring share, a tier, a directory. Two platform rules shape the route. Upwork's 24-month non-circumvention term and Fiverr's ban on taking work off the platform mean the partnership program must be joinable through email, LinkedIn, and Pico's own pages, with counsel's review of the terms in week 5 (D50).
