# Run 2, Lead Magnet: Agency AI Shopping Readiness Scorecard

The skill's Lead Magnet Specification for ScoreApp, built on the Run 2 demand driver, the **Paid Usability Session** (Dashboard 1, Step 1.6), and its scorecard variant. For implementers who serve US stores. Built on 2026-11-03. Implementation copy is in `scorecard_spec.docx`.

Rules applied: the score is the implementer's self-assessment, and every result message says so. No income, lead, or traffic figure is promised. The session fee is $150 and the partner terms are 25% for 24 months (D33, D35). The day 2 email uses a labelled sandbox example until a case study clears the intake rule. The scorecard goes live in week 6, with the partner page. The scorecard lives on Pico's own page or ScoreApp, never inside Upwork or Fiverr.

## Sheet: Overview

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| Scorecard Name | Agency AI Shopping Readiness Scorecard |
| Target ICP | Run 2 Levels 1 and 2 (Dashboard 4): freelancers and small studios, then build and CRO agencies, serving US stores |
| Problem it Reveals | Whether the implementer has a tested, maintainable answer when clients ask for AI shopping help: setup without theme edits, something to show the client, and a way to be paid for evaluation time |
| Immediate Value to User | A score from 0 to 100, a tier, three next steps, and the Client Store Findability Audit template |
| Conversion Path to Pico | Scorecard, then results with three next steps, then the audit template, then the sandbox and the setup skill, then an application for a paid usability session, then a case study and the partnership waitlist |

## Sheet: Questions

Higher score means a bigger readiness gap, and a stronger fit for the paid session. Points add to a maximum of 100.

| # | Question | Answer Options (4-5 options) | Scoring Logic | What It Reveals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How many client stores do you set up or maintain in a year? | 0 to 2. 3 to 10. 11 to 30. More than 30 | 2, 8, 12, 15 | Repeat deployment potential, and how much a multi-store workspace would matter |
| 2 | How often do clients ask you about AI chat or AI shopping assistants? | Never. Once or twice. Every few months. On most new projects | 0, 5, 10, 15 | Client demand the implementer is already hearing |
| 3 | What do you recommend today when they ask? | We don't offer anything yet. Shopify's free Inbox agent. A helpdesk or chat app we already use. A custom build. Not sure | 15, 8, 6, 10, 12 | The current answer, and how settled it is |
| 4 | How do you add apps to client themes? | Only through app embeds and app blocks. A mix of embeds and code edits. Mostly code edits. Not sure | 5, 10, 15, 8 | Theme risk, which a theme app embed removes |
| 5 | Do you use a coding agent such as Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, or Antigravity in Shopify work? | Daily. Sometimes. Tried it. No | 15, 10, 5, 2 | Fit for Pico's setup skill |
| 6 | What can you show clients about what their shoppers ask for? | A monthly report we produce. Search terms from Shopify's analytics. Only what support tickets say. Nothing yet | 2, 6, 12, 15 | Whether there is anything concrete to show the client, vector 4 in Dashboard 2 |
| 7 | How are you paid for time spent evaluating new apps? | A vendor pays us. Clients pay for evaluation time. We absorb the time | 0, 5, 10 | Whether a paid session changes their economics, vector 3 in Dashboard 2 |
