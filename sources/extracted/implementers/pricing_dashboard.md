Competitor programs appear for structure and as the floor Pico's terms sit above, never as Pico's numbers, and they come from `research/2026-11-03-step-0g-partner-programs.md`, read through the web search index, with conflicts shown. `DERIVED` marks arithmetic done here. ## Sheet: Value_Metric

### Step 9.1: what to pay implementers for

| Denominator | Who pays this way, 2026-11-03 | Correlation to Value | Scalability | Ease of Measurement | Score (1-10) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Per session: the engagement fee | No app vendor found. Research platforms pay participants | High at launch: it buys installs and feedback before any referral exists | Low: a cost for every session | Easy: a fixed scope paid on the platform | 7 |
| Share of a referred store's subscription | Gorgias, Tidio, Rep AI, Octane AI, Okendo, the Shopify Partner Program | High: income follows stores that keep paying | High | Medium: the referral must be attributed. Shopify Billing records each store's charges | 8 |
| Managed share: per store the implementer actively manages | Rebuy (5% while the agency works with the brand) | High: pays for ongoing work, which agencies sell | High | Medium to hard: needs a record of who manages which store | 7 |
| One-time bounty per referral | Gorgias Elite (15%), Tidio (up to $27,000 per referral), Shopify ($2,500 per qualifying Plus referral) | Medium: rewards the introduction, not retention | Medium | Easy | 5 |
| Client discount the implementer passes on | Rep AI Elite (15%), LoyaltyLion (10% split) | Low to medium | Medium | Easy | 4 |
| Resale margin under the implementer's brand | Zipchat | Medium | High | Hard: billing and brand work | 3 |

### Recommendation

| Element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Primary partner metric | **A share of the subscriptions of stores an implementer refers or manages.** It matches the floor every program sets, and it pays only while the store keeps paying. **Decided (D35): 25% for 24 months from the store's first paid charge, 30% for lead partners.** One share per store |
| Launch metric | **A fee per paid usability session,** the owner's opening move. **Decided (D33): $150 for a fixed scope of up to 90 minutes,** plus an optional $75 interview (D34). It ends when the session ends and does not depend on a referral |
| Rationale | The session fee buys first installs and feedback. The recurring share keeps the implementer after the session. A 10% managed share pays on stores the partner manages but did not refer, recorded by deal registration (D35, D38). No bounty, discount, or resale: a bounty pays for installs that churn |
| Payout route | **Decided (D36):** session fees go through Upwork, Fiverr, or invoice, by where the implementer was found. Recurring shares are paid monthly on the 15th for charges Shopify paid out to Pico in the prior month, with a $50 minimum carried forward, by PayPal or bank transfer. A ledger until 25 partners, then a partner platform such as the ones programs found use. Partners met on Upwork are paid through Upwork for 24 months (D49) |
| Development store policy | **Decided (D22): "free for testing" for paid plans on other partners' development stores,** switched on in Shopify App Pricing before outreach starts. A billing setting, low effort (Dashboard 1) |

### Anchor 3: breakeven, for Pico, internal

Written as arithmetic on the decided inputs. Pico's net revenue per store uses the public plan prices and Shopify's revenue share, 0% on the first $1M of lifetime app revenue and 15% above it.

| Item | Formula | Inputs, decided 2026-11-03 |
|---|---|---|
| Cost of one paid session | Session fee plus the platform fee on the buyer side | $150. Upwork planned at 8% plus up to $14.99 per contract, about $177. Fiverr 5.5% plus $3.50, about $162. Invoice about $152 (D31, D33) |
| Pico's net from one referred store, per year | Plan price times 12, less Shopify's revenue share, less the partner's share | Growth: $588, less 0% to Shopify below $1M lifetime, less 25%: $441 (`DERIVED`) |
| Sessions paid for by one store | Net per store per year divided by the cost of one session | $441 divided by $177: about 2.5 Upwork sessions (`DERIVED`) |
| Stores needed per session to break even | 1 divided by "sessions paid for by one store" | About 0.4 of a Growth store kept for a year (`DERIVED`) |

### "One referred store pays for X sessions"

**Message, internal only:** "One store on Growth, kept for a year, pays for about 2.5 paid sessions on Upwork." `DERIVED`, before model cost. At the $0.02 ceiling, model use on Growth is $144 a year, which leaves $297, about 1.7 sessions (D28).

| Key | Item | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| fee | Session fee paid to the implementer, $ | 150 | Decided, D33 |
| platform_rate | Platform fee on the buyer side, as a decimal | 0.08 | Upwork planning rate. Use 0.055 for Fiverr, 0 for invoice |
| platform_flat | Platform flat fee per order, $ | 14.99 | Upwork contract fee, upper bound. Use 3.50 for Fiverr |
| plan_price | Plan price per month, $ | 49 | Growth. Use 99 for Pro |
| months | Months the store stays | 12 | A one-year scenario. The share runs for up to 24 |
| partner_share | Partner's recurring share, as a decimal | 0.25 | Decided, D35. Use 0.30 for lead partners |
| shopify_share | Shopify's revenue share, as a decimal | 0 | 0 below $1M lifetime app revenue, 0.15 above |
| session_cost | Cost of one paid session, $ | `=IF(ISNUMBER({fee}),{fee}*(1+IF(ISNUMBER({platform_rate}),{platform_rate},0))+IF(ISNUMBER({platform_flat}),{platform_flat},0),"enter the fee")` | Fee plus platform fees |
| store_gross | Store revenue to Pico over the months entered, $ | `=IF(ISNUMBER({months}),{plan_price}*{months},"enter months")` | Before any share |
| partner_pay | Paid to the partner over those months, $ | `=IF(AND(ISNUMBER({months}),ISNUMBER({partner_share})),{plan_price}*{months}*(1-{shopify_share})*{partner_share},"enter months and share")` | A share of what Shopify pays out to Pico (D36) |
| store_net | Pico's net from the store over those months, $ | `=IF(AND(ISNUMBER({months}),ISNUMBER({partner_share})),{plan_price}*{months}*(1-{shopify_share})*(1-{partner_share}),"enter months and share")` | After Shopify's share and the partner's share |
| sessions_covered | Paid sessions covered by one store's net | `=IF(AND(ISNUMBER({store_net}),ISNUMBER({session_cost}),{session_cost}>0),{store_net}/{session_cost},"enter the inputs")` | The "one store pays for X sessions" figure |
