# Example, Upwork job post (implementers, stages 1 to 4)

Status: proposed gold standard. Paid on Upwork. No partner program, outside link, or email request inside Upwork. Founder approval pending.

---

**Title:** Install and test an AI shopping assistant on a Shopify store you work on ($150 fixed)

**Description:**

We make Pico, an AI shopping assistant for Shopify stores. We want feedback from developers who install apps for real stores.

What you will do, up to 90 minutes:
1. Try the Pico sandbox store.
2. Install Pico on a Shopify development store, or a client store with the merchant's written permission. Setup is a four-step checklist through a theme app embed, or our setup skill if you use a coding agent.
3. Run the Shopper Words Test: type 10 to 20 phrases the store's shoppers might use, and read the Analytics page.
4. Log your setup time and fill in the feedback form: what worked, what broke, what was unclear.

Deliverables: the completed feedback form and setup-time log.

Fee: $150 fixed price, released on milestone completion.

Optional second milestone: a 30-minute interview about your setup, $75.

Skills: Shopify app setup, theme app extensions, the theme editor. Coding agent experience is a plus, not required.

---

## Why this works
- The scope, time box, deliverables, and fee are explicit, which is what freelancers on Upwork screen for.
- Everything stays on Upwork: payment, the interview milestone, and the conversation.
- It describes setup in narrowed terms, a checklist and a theme app embed, never "installs itself".
- It invites the store's own phrases, so the session produces findings the implementer can show their client.
