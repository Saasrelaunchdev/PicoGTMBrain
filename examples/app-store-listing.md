**App name:** Pico AI Shopping Assistant

**Subtitle:** Let shoppers describe what they need, and see what they asked for.

**Introduction:**
An AI shopping assistant for Shopify that answers from your own catalog. Shoppers describe what they want in plain language, and Pico replies with product cards from your synced catalog. You see what they asked for, including the requests that found nothing.

**Details:**

*For shoppers who don't know the product's name.* A shopper types "waterproof hiking boots under $150." An AI model reads the request, then Pico matches it to your synced catalog by keywords, synonyms, and price range, and shows product cards with name, price, and stock. When a request is too vague, Pico asks one clarifying question. One tap adds an in-stock variant to your store's own cart.

*See what your shoppers asked for.* The Analytics page shows how requests move from the phrase to products shown, clicked, and added to cart. It lists the phrases that returned no products or few clicks, and each one can point to a gap in your catalog or in how products are described.

*A low-risk start.* Try the sandbox store before you install anything. Free covers 50 conversations a month, and new accounts get a 14 day Growth trial with no card. Setup is four steps through a theme app embed, with no theme code to edit.

**Features:**
- Product cards from your synced catalog, with stock as of your last sync
- One clarifying question for vague requests
- Add to cart from the card, using your store's own cart
- Discovery funnel, top phrases, zero result and weak click phrases
- Replies in the shopper's language, widget text in 10 languages
- Voice controls: personality, warmth, length, tone guidelines
- Four-step setup checklist that confirms the widget was seen

## Why this works
- It leads with the shopper's own words and what the merchant gets to see, which is the insight vector Pico leads on.
- Every claim is checkable in under a minute in the sandbox, so it needs no reviews to be believable.
- It says "stock as of your last sync" and "one clarifying question", the narrowed forms, instead of the broad claims competitors use.
- It names no competitor and says nothing about Shopify's tools.
