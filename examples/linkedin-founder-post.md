Status: proposed gold standard, the method archetype. Posted by hand. Founder approval pending.

---

A Shopify app embed is off by default after install.

It stays active only on the theme where you switched it on. Change the theme, and the app can quietly disappear from the storefront.

That is the first thing we built around at Pico. Our setup is four steps: authorize, switch on the Pico Assistant embed, sync the catalog, and confirm a visitor actually saw the widget. The checklist does not tick the last box until someone has.

If you install apps for clients, you know which step gets skipped.

We're paying Shopify developers $150 to install Pico on a store they work on and tell us where the checklist still lets them down. Comment "session" and I'll send the brief.
