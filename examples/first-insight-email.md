# Example, first insight email (merchants, stage 5)

Status: proposed gold standard. The email ships when its trigger ships (IN BUILD, week 4). Values in brackets are filled from the store's own Analytics page at send time. Founder approval pending.

---

**From:** [named person] at Pico
**Subject:** [n] phrases your shoppers asked this week found nothing

Hi [first name],

In its first week on [store name], Pico had [n] conversations. Shoppers clicked a product in [n] of them and added to cart in [n].

These [n] requests returned no products:

- "[phrase 1]"
- "[phrase 2]"
- "[phrase 3]"

A zero result can mean a missing product, or a product described in words your shoppers don't use. One fix to try this week: add the words from "[phrase 1]" to the title or description of the product it should have found, then watch the phrase on the Analytics page next week.

[Open your Analytics page]

[Named person]
Pico, picofinds.com

You're receiving this because you installed Pico. [Unsubscribe]. [US postal address]

---

## Why this works
- The subject and first line are the store's own numbers and phrases, so the value is visible before any product talk.
- It explains a zero result honestly, two possible causes, which is the narrowed claim.
- One fix, one page, one link. Nothing about sales, because Pico does not measure them.
- It sets up the proof capture point: the first fall in a store's zero result rate after a fix.
