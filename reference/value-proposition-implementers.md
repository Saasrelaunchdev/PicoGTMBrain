# Value proposition, implementers

Load for any implementer email, LinkedIn message, job post, partner page, or one-pager. Distills the implementer Value Proposition Framework (2026-11-03). Body copy is 25 words or fewer. Proof headlines are open proof.

**Brand promise.** We pay you to try Pico on a store you work on, and we credit your work when your client agrees.

**Positioning.** An AI shopping assistant built for the people who set up stores: a sandbox and a setup skill that cut setup hours, an Analytics page the client can see, a paid first session, and case studies credited to the implementer.

- Shopify's developer documentation explains theme app embeds and what happens to app blocks on uninstall.

- The Sandbox link in picofinds.com's top menu runs a sample outdoor gear catalog, with a demo widget included.
- The Analytics page is on every plan, Free included, so zero result phrases can be shown to a client before anyone pays.
- The session brief states scope, fee, and payment route before you accept: a fixed-price milestone on Upwork, an order on Fiverr, or an invoice paid within 7 days.
