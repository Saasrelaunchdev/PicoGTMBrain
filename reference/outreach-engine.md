# Channel engine, implementers: cold email, LinkedIn, Upwork and Fiverr

Load first for any implementer outreach: emails, LinkedIn notes and posts, job posts, session kits, partner invitations. Synthesized from the implementer journey, assets library, platform rules research, and decisions D33 to D52.

## Thesis
Every message is an offer of paid work, not a pitch: $150 for up to 90 minutes on a store the implementer already works on. Freelancers already sell app installs for about $100, so a paid session reads as ordinary work. The partnership follows the session, never leads it.

## How the channels feed each other
Upwork brings applicants to a posted job (15 sessions). Fiverr starts from an existing gig or a brief (5). Cold email (6) and LinkedIn (4) reach agencies that do not sell on marketplaces. Every session produces findings for the client, an interview, a possible case study, and from week 6 a partner who joined through Pico's own pages.

## Platform rules, load-bearing
- **Cold email (CAN-SPAM):** accurate headers and subject, identified as an advertisement, the US postal address, an opt-out honored within 10 business days. US recipients only in the first 90 days. Then UK companies and LLPs only, never UK sole traders or ordinary partnerships, never cold to the EU.
- **LinkedIn:** messages sent by hand. No bots, no automation, no scraping tools.
- **Upwork:** relationships found there keep payments on Upwork for 24 months unless a conversion fee is paid. Partners met there are paid through Upwork for 24 months.
- **Fiverr:** payment only by a Fiverr order. No outside link meant to move the relationship, no email request, no partner invitation.
- Counsel reviews the partner terms, session brief, and conduct rules in week 5.

## Archetypes
- **The paid offer.** Scope and fee in the first line, deliverables, reply to book.
- **The method post.** One idea: install through a theme app embed, setup by coding agent, what a zero result phrase means.
- **The walkthrough.** A real recording on a development store, uninstall shown.
- **The findings handoff.** What the client's shoppers asked that found nothing, as a list the implementer can bill fixes against.
- **The invitation.** After a session: case study permission, then the partner terms quoted exactly.

## Plan, write, edit
1. **Plan.** One level (freelancer or agency), one stage, one channel, its platform rule.
2. **Write.** Under 120 words for a first email. Fee and scope first. Lines from `ops/copy-bank.md`.
3. **Edit.** Strip any income, traffic, certification, or white label language. Check the channel rule line by line.

## Calls to action
"Apply for a paid session." "Download the setup skill." "Read the technical one-pager." From week 6, off-platform only: "Read the terms on Pico's partner page."

## What good looks like
Cold email reply rate 5%, above the 3.43% average a 2026 report cites. LinkedIn acceptance 30%, replies from 15%. 10 qualified applicants per Upwork post, or the fee rises to $200. 30 sessions by day 60. Payment within 7 days of the feedback form, every time.

## Never in this channel
Automated LinkedIn sends, purchased lists used outside their terms, partner pitches inside Upwork or Fiverr, rates other than the published terms, named prospects in reports, Gulf agency outreach.
