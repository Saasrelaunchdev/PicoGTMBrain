# Partner program and implementer economics

Load for anything about paying implementers: session fees, the partner share, tiers, payouts, attribution, and partner FAQs. Distills the implementer Pricing and Packaging dashboard and D33 to D41. Everything here is IN BUILD: sessions from week 2, the partner page in week 6.

## What Pico pays for

| Element | Terms |
|---|---|
| Paid Usability Session | $150 for a fixed scope of up to 90 minutes. Pico pays the platform fees |
| Interview | $75 for 30 minutes, optional, after the session |
| Referred store share | 25% of the store's subscription charges for 24 months from its first paid charge |
| Lead partner share | 30% on referred stores from the month the partner reaches 5 active paying stores |
| Managed share | 10% on stores the partner manages but did not refer |
| One share per store | No stacking. No cash bounty, no resale margin, no client discount at launch |

## The ladder

| Tier | Entry | Pays | Also gets | Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Session participant | A completed paid session | $150, plus $75 for an interview | A partner invitation, a case study offer | 30 sessions by day 60 |
| Partner | An accepted application, open to anyone who completed a session or runs one active client store | 25% referred, 10% managed | A listing on picofinds.com/partners after the first active paying store, free development store testing | 15 partners by day 90 |
| Lead partner | 5 active paying stores, referred or managed | 30% referred, 10% managed | Co-marketing, a published case study, a tier badge, priority support | 3 by month 6 |
| Agency workspace | A partner with 3 or more active client stores | Free to the partner, each store pays its own plan | One workspace across client stores | Built when 10 partners qualify |

Good standing: at least one active paying store in the last 12 months, no platform rule breach, no incentivized reviews, copy that uses the narrowed claims. Earned shares continue to term unless a partner is removed for a breach.

## Payouts
Monthly on the 15th, for subscription charges Shopify paid out to Pico in the prior month. $50 minimum, carried forward. PayPal or bank transfer. Tax forms before the first payout. A ledger until 25 partners, then a partner platform. Partners met on Upwork are paid through Upwork for the first 24 months.

## Attribution
Deal registration: the partner registers the client store's myshopify domain before the install, the merchant confirms by email, and the first valid registration holds for 90 days. Checked against install and charge records in the Partner Dashboard.

## Referral traffic
Merchants who ask Pico for setup help, through "Book a tailored demo" or support, are offered a listed partner, in rotation and by country. Partners see the count each month. No volume is promised.

## The economics, internal
- One paid session costs about $177 on Upwork, $162 on Fiverr, $152 by invoice.
- One referred Growth store kept a year returns about $441 to Pico after a 25% share, before model cost, so it pays for about 2.5 Upwork sessions. After model cost at the ceiling, about 1.7.
- What implementers earn now: about $100 per AI chat app install. Pico: $150 for the first session, then 25% of the plan, $12.25 a month on Growth, $294 over 24 months, if the client keeps paying.
- Benchmarks, structure only: Gorgias 20% for 2 to 3 years, Rebuy 5% to 15% for 12 months plus 5% managed, Okendo 20%, Octane 20% for life, Shopify 20% of the merchant's subscription.

## Never say
"Earn recurring revenue", "guaranteed leads", "certified partner", "white label", any rate other than the published terms, or "Join the Pico Partner Program" before the page is live.
