# Value proposition, merchants

Load for any merchant headline, listing copy, landing page, email, or in-product string. Distills the merchant Value Proposition Framework (2026-11-03): promise, three pillars, nine reasons to believe, each with approved copy. Body copy is 25 words or fewer. Proof headlines are open proof: an invitation to check, never a customer result.

**Brand promise.** Shoppers find products from your catalog by asking in their own words, and you see what they asked for.

**Positioning.** Built around your synced catalog, with insight into what shoppers ask. Stated as what Pico is built around, never as something only Pico can do.

## Supporting proof points (open proof)
- The Sandbox link in picofinds.com's top menu runs a sample outdoor gear catalog on Pico's real API. Type a request with a price limit, then check the prices on the cards.
- Every card in the sandbox has a View link to the product it came from.
- Try a one-word request such as "boots" in the sandbox, then add a product to the cart. Out-of-stock items cannot be added.
- The Shopper Words Test worksheet lists ten phrases a shopper might use. Try them in the sandbox, then compare them with your own product titles.
- `[IN BUILD]` The plan page states each plan's quota, what counts as one conversation, the alerts at 80% and 100%, and that Pico never charges overage.
