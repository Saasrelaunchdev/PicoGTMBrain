## The runway gate
Spend is released in 30-day blocks. Each block needs at least 6 months of company runway after it. If a block fails, paid sessions pause and outreach continues on email, LinkedIn, and the scorecard.

## Slip rule
If any build list item slips by more than two weeks, the founder and engineer move the next block's scope, and the change is a decision entry.

## Feedback cadence
Founding merchants: a monthly 30-minute call. Partners: a quarterly call and a findings digest after each batch of 10 sessions.
