# The GCC, a secondary go-to-market for later

Load only when someone asks about the Gulf, Arabic-market copy, or the GCC trigger. Nothing here is pursued now, and no GCC copy, channel, or budget exists in the primary plan. Distills the GCC sheets of both ICP dashboards and the Arabic markets research (2026-11-03).

## The trigger
The GCC opens when the US founding cohort is full and at least 5 GCC stores have asked for Pico in one quarter (D11). `innovation/tripwires.json` watches for it: log every GCC inbound as a `SEGMENT` signal with `region: GCC`. When it fires, size the GCC from the UAE first and review messaging channels (D14).

## Merchants there
- Live Shopify stores: UAE 26,986, Saudi Arabia 4,480, Kuwait 3,790, Qatar 2,265, Bahrain at least 367, Oman at least 471. At least about 38,400 across the six (StoreLeads, 2026). Many UAE stores run English storefronts. Stores with an Arabic storefront are not published by any tracker, so it is asked at intake.
- Platform context: Saudi Arabia is mostly WooCommerce (36.8%), Zid (16.4%), and Shopify (13.5%) of detected sites, and Salla leads by store count elsewhere. Pico reaches Salla and Zid only through the custom catalog API.
- How shoppers ask: social and messaging heavy. 79% of UAE shoppers use social media to research products (secondhand). Pico has no WhatsApp channel.
- Competition: Arabic-first Shopify apps (Luma, shamz.ai), Verifast naming Arabic, Fin and Lyro answering in Arabic, Salla's own Arabic AI search, Meta's business agent on WhatsApp.
- Billing: Shopify Billing in USD, with no local-currency estimate for AED, SAR, KWD, or QAR.
- ICP if opened: UAE Shopify stores with Arabic or bilingual storefronts in the same help-me-choose categories.

## Implementers there
Partner Directory profiles in Dubai list Arabic right to left development as a service. The five UAE agency pages in the stack sample list local payment and WhatsApp setup, not chat apps. A Gulf agency can join the partner program through Pico's public page at any time, but there is no outreach.

## The one copy line, for later
"Arabic replies display right to left, with a language switcher and separate merchant copy per language." Merchant statement 1 and implementer statement 2 gain it when the GCC opens, and merchant pillar 1 gains a reason to believe.
