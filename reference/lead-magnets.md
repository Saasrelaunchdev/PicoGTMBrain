# Lead magnets, both audiences

Load to build, edit, or reply to anything around the two ScoreApp scorecards. Distills both lead magnet specifications and scorecard briefs (2026-11-03). Every result message says the score is a self-assessment. No industry statistic is quoted.

## Shopify Store Findability Scorecard (merchants, live week 3)

**Reveals** whether shoppers who describe a product in their own words are likely to find it: catalog size and likeness, the words in product titles, how pre-purchase questions arrive, and whether the merchant can see what shoppers asked.

**Seven questions, 100 points, higher means more findability risk:** products sold (0 to 15), how alike they are (0 to 15), what shoppers ask before buying (0 to 15), whether they read "Searches with no results" in Shopify's search analytics (0 to 15), whether titles use shoppers' words (0 to 15), how shoppers without the product name find it (3 to 15), whether they can see what shoppers asked (0 to 10).

| Score | Tier | CTA |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 39 | Shoppers can find their way | Get the Shopper Words Test worksheet |
| 40 to 69 | Some shoppers may be getting lost | Try your phrases in the Pico sandbox store |
| 70 to 100 | Shoppers ask in words your catalog doesn't use | Install Pico free, or apply for the founding cohort |

**Landing page.** Headline: "How easily can shoppers find your products in their own words?" Subhead: "Seven questions, a score from 0 to 100, and three fixes you can make this week." CTA: "Get my Findability Score". Footer: "Your score is your own assessment, based on your answers. It is not a measurement of your store."

**Emails, plain text, one CTA, 60 words or fewer:** day 0 the score and three fixes, day 2 ten phrases with a labeled sandbox example, day 5 what Pico does and does not do, day 8 the founding cohort, which closes at 20 stores or 90 days after the listing is live. No invented deadline.

**Routing.** 70 to 100 goes to the founder for the founding cohort. Tag by tier and by answers to questions 3 and 7.

## Agency AI Shopping Readiness Scorecard (implementers, live week 6)

**Reveals** whether the implementer has a tested, maintainable answer when clients ask for AI shopping help: setup without theme edits, something to show the client, and a way to be paid for evaluation time.

**Seven questions on stack, client base, and AI work, higher means a bigger readiness gap** and a stronger fit for the paid session. Question 1 counts client stores set up or maintained in a year.

**Path.** Score, three next steps, the Client Store Findability Audit template, the sandbox and setup skill, an application for a paid session, then a case study and the partnership.

**Landing page.** Headline: "When a client asks for AI shopping help, how ready is your answer?" Subhead: "Seven questions for Shopify developers and agencies, a score from 0 to 100, and three next steps." CTA: "Get my readiness score".

**Emails:** day 0 the score and next steps, day 2 what a zero result phrase tells a client, day 5 paid to test it on a store you work on, day 8 the session cohort and the partner program, sent by email only.
