## What reviewers say across the set
- Praise: hands-on vendor support, easy setup, AI that resolves most questions.
- Complaints, most frequent first: pricing or billing surprise (16+), setup or theme conflict (12+), support responsiveness (8+), made-up answers (7+), results not arriving (7+).
- Frequency counts are reviews seen in indexed snippets, a floor rather than a census.

## Where each leader wins acquisition
- Setup (Chatty): five-star reviews drive App Store ranking, and 1,900 reviews compound.
- Support breadth (Tidio): merchants buy one support tool and get shopping help with it.
- Insight (Pico, by capability): a merchant who sees the requests that found nothing has a reason to install before believing any sales claim. The Shopper Words Test turns this into the entry point.
- Traceability (Pico, narrowly): reviewers distrust answers they cannot check. Cards from the synced catalog plus a visible sync log give the merchant something to verify.
