# Competitor battlecards

Load for sales calls, partner calls, review replies, and objection prep. Three lines per rival: their real strength first, their real gap, how Pico wins. Internal only. In copy that customers see, never name a rival and never say what a Shopify tool lacks.

## Merchants

**Shopify Inbox with the Inbox agent (the default comparison)**
- Strength: free, native, on about 358,000 stores, personalizes from Shop sign-in, and now starts checkout from chat.
- Gap: built for support breadth across orders and policies. Pico does not try to match it there.
- How Pico wins: the merchant sees what shoppers asked, the phrases that found nothing, checkable on the free plan. Pico costs more, and the case rests on what the merchant learns.

**Chatty**
- Strength: responsive support, easy setup, a 50-conversation free plan, about 1,900 reviews, selling plus helpdesk in one app.
- Gap: reviewers cite billing surprises, and AI conversations cost about $0.14 to $0.20 with $0.40 overage.
- How Pico wins: a quota the merchant can see and, once shipped, a pause instead of overage. Insight into requests instead of a support inbox.

**Shopify Search & Discovery**
- Strength: free, in every store, semantic search for eligible stores, search-bar zero-result reports.
- Gap: built for the search bar. Shoppers who don't know a product's name have nowhere to describe it.
- How Pico wins: a conversation beside search, never instead of it. Speak well of the native report and use it in the Shopper Words Test.

**Tidio with Lyro**
- Strength: established support suite, 48 languages, about 27,500 stores, Lyro resolves most questions.
- Gap: reviewers report Lyro limits and billing disputes, and slow email support.
- How Pico wins: pre-purchase only, priced per conversation at a lower unit, with hands-on onboarding for the founding cohort.

**Build or hire**
- Strength: full control, a person can answer anything.
- Gap: the merchant carries model cost, upkeep, migrations, and staffing.
- How Pico wins: an App Store install with a four-step checklist, nothing to build or maintain.

**Shopify agentic stack**
- Strength: reach inside AI assistants by default, no channel fees per Shopify.
- Gap: the conversation happens off the store.
- How Pico wins: Pico is the conversation on the store, with the insight that comes from it. Complementary, never framed as a fight.

## Implementers

**Gorgias agency program**
- Strength: tiers agencies display, a partner manager, co-marketing, lead sharing, 20% for 2 to 3 years.
- Gap: the share freezes without a closed deal a year, and client billing surprises land on the agency.
- How Pico wins: paid before any referral, published terms, 25% for 24 months with good standing at one active paying store a year.

**Shopify Inbox**
- Strength: free, native, a one-sentence recommendation with no upkeep.
- Gap: the implementer earns nothing from it.
- How Pico wins: a paid first session, then a share on each referred store, plus something concrete to show the client.

**Rebuy plus Search & Discovery**
- Strength: pays a 5% managed share whoever referred the brand, and sits in more sampled stacks than any other discovery app.
- Gap: recommendations from behavior, not from what shoppers say.
- How Pico wins: client-visible phrases shoppers asked, in their own words, as CRO research input, and a 10% managed share.

**Klaviyo Customer Agent**
- Strength: already in 17 of 26 sampled stacks, client health dashboards.
- Gap: requirements grow with each tier.
- How Pico wins: one threshold to lead partner, 5 active paying stores, and a paid session that needs no tier at all.

**Build with the AI Toolkit**
- Strength: billable, fully owned work, with Shopify's own tools.
- Gap: maintenance and migrations stay with the builder.
- How Pico wins: four steps or a coding agent, with no build to maintain after handover, and the setup hours are paid in the session.

**Shopify Partner Program**
- Strength: every implementer is in it, and its directory is where merchants find agencies.
- Gap: commission confusion reported in community threads.
- How Pico wins: payouts on the 15th with a $50 minimum and deal registration the partner can check. Separate from Shopify's own earnings, which are unaffected.
