# Marketing campaigns and asset backlog

Load to plan, prioritize, or produce an asset. Distills both Assets and Collaterals Libraries (2026-11-03). Each audience runs three channels, and its demand driver is the top-of-funnel asset in every one. Timeframes are production estimates for a two-person team.

## Held back, and why
- Paid ads: an unproven offer with no conversion data buys expensive evidence. Revisit once the listing converts.
- Cold outbound to store owners: with an empty proof log, a cold message can only describe features.
- LinkedIn automation and LinkedIn Ads: barred by LinkedIn's User Agreement, and replaced by marketplace posts.
- A partner pitch inside Upwork or Fiverr: platform rules. The partner page is public.
- GCC and Arabic-market assets, and Gulf agency outreach: for later, on the trigger in `gcc-later.md`.

## Production rules for every asset
Claims in narrowed wording only. Sample data labeled as sample. No competitor named. Shopify never disparaged. One call to action. Run `ops/QA-checklist.md`, then list the asset in `ops/asset-index.md` and log an `asset.shipped` event.
