# ICP and personas, merchants

Load for targeting, segmentation, persona-specific copy, and founding cohort intake. Distills the merchant ICP Dashboard and Buying Committee (2026-11-03). Bands come from `ops/decisions.md` D53 and D54. Counts: `market-sizing.md`.

## The two levels

| | Level 1: no conversational discovery yet | Level 2: started and stalled |
|---|---|---|
| Segment | US Shopify stores whose shoppers need help choosing between similar or technical products, on Shopify's own search, no AI assistant | Growing brands already paying for a search, quiz, or chat app whose shoppers still miss products |
| Categories | Health, sports and outdoor, pets, auto parts and fitment, consumer electronics. Home and garden adjacent | The same, plus home and garden and beauty |
| Bands (planning) | 100+ products, 10,000 to 100,000 sessions a month, 1 to 10 people, about $150,000 to $3 million a year, Basic or Grow | 250+ products, 100,000 to 1 million sessions, 10 to 50 people plus an agency, about $1.5 to $30 million, Advanced or Plus |
| Fit check | 2% of sessions start a conversation: 200 to 2,000 a month, Growth or Pro | 2,000 to 20,000 a month, Pro then Scale, then custom |
| Pain | Shoppers who don't know a product's name leave or email. The owner answers the same questions by hand. Wary of app bills that grow | A paid search or quiz app still leaves requests unmatched. Reports don't show what shoppers asked an assistant. Too many bills |
| Triggers | Repeat pre-purchase questions, a new product line, opening "Searches with no results", peak season prep, a redesign, Shopify AI news | Switching chat apps, a search or quiz app renewal, a replatform, a new market, an agency recommendation |
| Engagement | Self-serve: sandbox, App Store install, checklist, Free, 14 day trial | Agency-installed or assisted onboarding through the founding cohort and implementer partners |

## The buying committee

**Level 1 collapses into one person.** The owner installs, approves the charge in Shopify Billing, and cancels by uninstalling. Blocks: fear of a growing bill, setup time, wrong answers to shoppers, doubt it beats the free Inbox agent, theme risk, no reviews yet. Drivers: fewer repeat questions, shoppers finding products alone, seeing what shoppers ask, no card to start. Buy-in assets: the sandbox, listing screenshots of cards and Analytics, the checklist, a plan page that states each quota, the Shopper Words Test. Channels: App Store search and the AI Shopping Assistants collection, Google and AI answer engines, roundups, Shopify Community, their freelancer, YouTube (to test).

**Level 2 holds, plus the implementer.**

| | User | Manager | Decision maker | Implementer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Titles | Ecommerce manager, merchandiser, CX lead | Head of ecommerce, growth or CRO lead | Founder, CEO, GM | Agency or freelance developer, theme developer |
| Influence | Shortlists, runs the trial | Approves the trial and spend | Signs, can veto | Rarely signs, can block by never enabling the embed |
| Stages | 2 to 5 | 1, 3, 5, 6 | 3, 4, 7 | 3, 4, 7 |
| Blocks | Another dashboard, catalog fix work, clashes with existing apps | No revenue attribution, overlap with paid tools, a vendor with no reviews | Cost without proof of sales, "Shopify's free agent is good enough" | Theme conflicts, speed impact, uninstall residue |
| Drivers | A list of catalog fixes, something to report upward | Evidence for merchandising, fewer tools | A low-risk start, a flat price with a stated quota | No theme code, a setup skill, a sandbox, the partner share |
| Assets | Sandbox, Analytics tour, checklist, the "not offered" list | Trial Analytics, the breakeven worksheet, plan comparison | A one-page summary with the breakeven arithmetic | The setup skill, the technical one-pager, the paid session |

## Design partners: the founding cohort
20 US stores in the five categories, 100+ products, on Basic, Grow, or Advanced, run by someone who opens their own analytics and will install an app with no reviews. They get hands-on onboarding, a direct line to the founder, roadmap input, and 50% off any paid plan for 6 billing periods. They give feedback on setup and Analytics, and permission to cite outcomes only if they agree.

Success metrics, measured by Pico only: widget seen within 24 hours, first conversation within 7 days, 3 catalog fixes from the report in 60 days, and the zero result rate on requests in days 31 to 60 against days 1 to 30 as the store's own baseline. No sales figure is a success metric until revenue attribution exists.

Sources: Shopper Words Test and the Findability Scorecard, the App Store listing, client stores brought by implementer partners. Opens 2026-12-01, closes at 20 stores or 90 days after the listing goes live. Case study request in writing at day 60.

Early adopter traits: tries new apps, answers email, joins a monthly 30-minute call, comfortable being told the product is early.
