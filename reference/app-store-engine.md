# Channel engine, merchants: App Store, content, and product-led

Load first for any merchant channel task: listing copy, screenshots, catalog guides, comparison pages, lifecycle email, in-product strings. Synthesized from the merchant journey, assets library, and listing decisions.

## Thesis
Merchants decide on the App Store, in AI answer engines, and inside the trial. Pico has no reviews, so every channel hands the merchant something to check before trusting a claim: the sandbox, their own shoppers' words, the Analytics page. Activation, not awareness, decides whether an install becomes a paying store.

## How the three channels feed each other
Content and search bring merchants who already suspect shoppers miss products, and send them to the sandbox. The listing converts them with screenshots of the Shopper Words Test, cards, Analytics, and the quota. The product-led lifecycle carries them from install to widget seen, first conversation, first fix, and paid plan. Every fix and review feeds the listing's credibility.

## Archetypes to rotate
- **Words gap.** A shopper phrase, the product title it missed, the fix. Built only from sandbox or permissioned data.
- **Category guide.** How shoppers in health, outdoor, pets, auto parts, or electronics describe products, and how to name products so they are found.
- **When Pico fits and when it doesn't.** Honest comparison pages that name what Pico does not do. Facts sourced and dated.
- **Setup proof.** Four steps, the theme app embed, the checklist, the speed number once measured.
- **Quota clarity.** What a conversation is, what happens at the quota. The pause line only once shipped.

## The listing, field by field
Name "Pico AI Shopping Assistant". Category Chat. Search terms: AI shopping assistant, product finder, product discovery. Screenshot order: Shopper Words Test, product cards, Analytics, plan page with the quota. Introduction leads with the brand promise. Benefits follow the three pillars. Pricing states each plan's quota and, once shipped, the conversation definition and the no-overage rule. Demo store field: the sandbox.

## Plan, write, edit
1. **Plan.** One audience level, one stage, one pillar, one CTA. Pick the archetype.
2. **Write.** Start from `ops/copy-bank.md` lines. Name pages. Use sandbox examples labeled as sample.
3. **Edit.** Cut every sentence that would still read correctly with a competitor's name in it. Check every capability against `rules/feature-status.md`, every superlative against `rules/do-not-say.md`.

## Hook patterns
- The shopper's words: "waterproof hiking boots under $150".
- The merchant's own report: "Open 'Searches with no results' in your Shopify admin."
- The checkable claim: "Open any card and check it against the catalog."

## Calls to action
Before the listing is live: "Connect your store". After: "Install free from the Shopify App Store". Evaluation: "Open the sandbox store". One per asset.

## What good looks like
Listing visit to install 3%. Sandbox visit to install 10%. Widget seen within 24 hours on 65% of installs. First conversation within 7 days on 50%. Trial to paid 15% by day 30. Reviews asked only after the merchant has opened Analytics and made one fix. Review a channel on a full measurement cycle, never a single week.

## Never in this channel
Review incentives, fake urgency, outcome numbers, named competitors on the listing, Arabic or GCC screenshots, and paid ads.
