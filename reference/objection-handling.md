# Objection handling

Load for sales calls, partner calls, review replies, support macros, and FAQ pages. Derived from the buying committee blockers, the competitive weaknesses, and the claim ledger. Headings are in the buyer's words. Where the honest answer is "not yet", it says not yet.

## Merchants

**"Why not just turn on Shopify's free Inbox agent?"**
You can, and it is free. Pico costs more. The reason to install is what you learn: the phrases your shoppers asked that returned nothing or drew few clicks. Try it in the sandbox, then on the Free plan, before paying anything.

**"Will it tell my shoppers something wrong?"**
Product cards show names, prices, and stock from your synced catalog, and the Catalog page shows the last successful sync. The AI writes the reply text, so read a few conversations in your first week. Stock is as of your last sync.

**"Will it break my theme or slow my store?"**
Pico runs as a theme app embed called Pico Assistant, so there is no theme code to edit, and Shopify removes embed blocks on uninstall. The speed test on Dawn is being measured now, and the number will be on the technical one-pager. We do not claim zero impact.

**"What happens when I hit the quota? I've been burned by app bills."**
Today the Plan & Usage page shows a quota meter. Coming soon: alerts at 80% and 100%, a 10% grace allowance on paid plans, then a pause until the next cycle or an upgrade. No overage charge, ever. A conversation is a thread with at least one AI reply, counted once per shopper and device per 24 hours.

**"Does it increase sales?"**
Pico does not measure revenue yet, and the dashboard says so. It measures what shoppers asked, what they were shown, what they clicked, and what they added to cart. Revenue attribution is on the roadmap.

**"Can it answer order and returns questions, or hand off to my team?"**
No. Pico answers product questions before purchase. Keep your help desk for orders and people.

**"You have no reviews."**
Correct, Pico is new. That is why the sandbox exists and why the Free plan includes every feature, so you can check every claim yourself. The founding cohort gets hands-on onboarding and a direct line to the founder.

**"Is it semantic search?"**
No. An AI model reads the request, then Pico matches it to your catalog by keywords, synonyms, and price range. If a request uses words your catalog doesn't, it shows up as a zero result phrase, which is the point of the report.

## Implementers

**"Is the $150 real, and when do I get paid?"**
Yes. The session brief states scope, fee, and payment route before you accept. On Upwork it is a fixed-price milestone, on Fiverr an order, otherwise an invoice paid within 7 days of the completed feedback form.

**"Will this break my client's store?"**
Start in the sandbox or on a development store. On a client store, the merchant agrees in writing first. Install is a theme app embed, removed on uninstall.

**"Your partner program has no track record."**
True. The terms are decided and published on picofinds.com/partners from week 6: 25% of each referred store's subscription for 24 months, 30% at five active paying stores, payouts on the 15th. No partner has been paid yet, so watch the first payout.

**"Why should I move my client off Gorgias or Klaviyo?"**
You shouldn't. Pico does product discovery before purchase, beside their stack. Show the client the phrases their shoppers asked that found nothing, and let them decide.

**"Can you guarantee me leads?"**
No. Merchants who ask Pico for setup help are offered listed partners in rotation, and you see the count each month. No volume is promised.

**"Do I need one login per client?"**
Today, yes: each plan connects one store, and you take the admin role on each client's account. A multi-store workspace is built once 10 partners each have 3 or more active paying stores.

**"Can I white label it?"**
No. There is no white label or resale.
