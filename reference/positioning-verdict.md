# Positioning verdicts, the settled forks

Load when someone asks why Pico leads on what it leads on, or proposes leading on something else. Distills the two positioning summaries and the competitive vectors, 2026-11-03.

## Merchants: shopper insight over support automation

**Verdict.** Pico positions on insight into what shoppers ask and on answers traceable to the synced catalog. It does not compete on support automation breadth, where AI sales and support agents lead.

**Why.** Those are the two vectors where the slate scores lowest. The first conversation with a merchant changes from "trust our lift number" to "see what your shoppers asked for", which the merchant can check in the sandbox and then on their own store on the free plan. It answers the complaints reviewers repeat after billing surprises: answers they cannot check, and results they never see. Every install is a choice against a free native agent on about 358,000 stores, so Pico wins on what the merchant learns, not on what the assistant can do.

| Vector | Leader | Pico now, target | Edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy setup with responsive help | Chatty 8.9 | 6.8, 7.9 | Competitors |
| Support automation breadth | Tidio 8.9 | 1.3, 1.3 by design | Competitors |
| Insight into what shoppers ask | Pico 8.3 | 8.3, 9.1 | Pico |
| Answers traceable to the catalog | Pico 7.7, Search & Discovery 7.1 | 7.7, 8.4 | Pico, narrowly |

**Defensibility.** Narrow and copyable: Shopify reports search-bar zero results, and Tidio lists questions Lyro could not answer, so a conversational report is one release away for either. The defensible part is the loop after the report: each zero result phrase becomes a catalog fix, and Pico shows whether the fix worked (holdout measurement, the proof capture point), plus the request history each store builds. If Pico ships that loop first and implementers adopt it as their audit tool, the lead compounds. If it stays a report, it is a feature, and it will be matched.

**What moves the targets.** Setup: hands-on founding cohort onboarding and implementers installing for stores. Insight: holdout measurement and the proof capture point. Traceability: showing in Analytics which catalog products each answer used.

## Implementers: paid first over partner economics

**Verdict.** Pico positions on paying implementers from the first engagement and on proof the implementer co-owns. It does not compete on established partner economics or stack presence, where the programs agencies already know lead.

**Why.** Every program on the slate pays after a referral or a closed deal. Paying for a usability session on first contact turns cold email, LinkedIn messages, and marketplace job posts into offers of paid work rather than pitches, which fits how freelancers already sell: app installs for about $100, research sessions benchmarked at $100 to $150 an hour. One partner serves 2 to 10 client stores as a freelancer, 10 to 100 as an agency (planning bands), so one partner can mean repeat deployments.

| Vector | Leader | Pico now, target | Edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Established partner economics | Shopify Partner Program 9.1 | 0.4, 4.6 | Competitors |
| Presence in the stack agencies sell | Klaviyo 9.3 | 0.2, 2.1 | Competitors |
| Paid from the first engagement | Build 6.9 | 0.0, 8.8 | Pico, by design |
| Proof the implementer co-owns | Build 6.8 | 1.1, 8.3 | Pico, by design |

**Defensibility.** Paying for sessions is easy to copy. What compounds is what sessions produce: case studies owned jointly and gated by permission, and implementers who have put Pico on several client stores, which needs the multi-store workspace once 10 partners each have 3 or more active stores. The gap between the first session and the partnership must close fast, because Gorgias, Klaviyo, and the Shopify Partner Program set the floor: a share, a tier, a directory. Upwork's 24-month term and Fiverr's off-platform ban mean the program must be joinable through email, LinkedIn, and Pico's own pages.

## Decided around both verdicts
- Two standalone go-to-markets, never blended in one asset.
- USA first, the GCC on a trigger (`gcc-later.md`).
- Open proof until the proof log has its first entry.
- A slate competitor shipping a conversational zero-result report reopens the merchant verdict's wording, not its direction: lead with the fix loop.
