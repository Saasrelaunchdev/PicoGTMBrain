# Market sizing, both audiences

Load for board, investor, or planning questions about how big each segment is. These are frames, not market sizes. Each figure keeps its own source, method, and date, and figures from different trackers are never added together. Never quote a sizing number in customer copy.

## Where the numbers get replaced
Merchant bands by the founding cohort's intake answers and first 30 days. The 2% conversation start rate by the cohort's first 30 days. Implementer bands by the first 30 session applications. Every replacement is a `metric.observed` event and a decision entry.
