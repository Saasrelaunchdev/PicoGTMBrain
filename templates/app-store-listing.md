# Template, Shopify App Store listing

Audience: merchants only. Stages 2 and 3. Channel engine: `reference/app-store-engine.md`. Voice reference: `examples/app-store-listing.md`.

## Fields, in Shopify's order
1. **App name.** "Pico AI Shopping Assistant". Never shortened.
2. **App card subtitle.** One line from the copy bank's merchant headlines, under the field limit.
3. **App introduction.** The brand promise, then what Pico is in one sentence. No superlative.
4. **App details.** Three short paragraphs, one per pillar: shoppers ask in their own words, see what your shoppers asked for, a low-risk start.
5. **Feature list.** Shipped capabilities only, each under the field limit, named as in `rules/glossary.md`.
6. **Screenshots, in this order.** The Shopper Words Test (a request, the cards it returned, the zero result phrase), product cards, the Analytics page, the plan page with each quota. Sandbox data, labeled as a sample store.
7. **Demo store.** The sandbox store.
8. **Pricing.** Free 50, Growth $49 for 600, Pro $99 for 2,000. Add Scale and yearly prices only once SHIPPED. Add the conversation definition and the no-overage rule only once SHIPPED.
9. **Category and search terms.** Chat. AI shopping assistant, product finder, product discovery.
10. **Support and links.** us@picofinds.com and picofinds.com.

## Fill-in checklist
- [ ] Every capability in the listing is SHIPPED in `rules/feature-status.md`.
- [ ] No review, install, or merchant count. No testimonial.
- [ ] No competitor named, nothing about what a Shopify tool lacks.
- [ ] Stock described as of the last catalog sync.
- [ ] Screenshots show sample data, labeled.

Guardrails: one audience, open proof only, `rules/do-not-say.md` on every line, `ops/QA-checklist.md` before submission.
