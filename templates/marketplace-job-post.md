# Template, Upwork job post and Fiverr brief

Audience: implementers. Stages 1 to 4. Paid usability work only. Channel engine: `reference/outreach-engine.md`. Voice reference: `examples/upwork-job-post.md`.

## Upwork fixed-price job post
1. **Title.** "Install and test an AI shopping assistant on a Shopify store you work on ($150 fixed)".
2. **What you will do.** Try the sandbox store. Install Pico on a development store, or a client store with the merchant's written permission. Run the Shopper Words Test with phrases from the store's customers. Log setup time. Fill in the feedback form.
3. **Deliverables.** The completed feedback form and setup-time log.
4. **Time.** Up to 90 minutes.
5. **Fee.** $150 fixed price, paid through Upwork on milestone completion.
6. **Skills.** Shopify app setup, theme app extensions, theme editor.
7. **Optional second milestone.** A 30-minute interview for $75.

## Fiverr order or brief
Order an existing Shopify app setup gig, or post a brief with the same scope and deliverables written as the order requirements. Paid by a Fiverr order.

## Session kit, sent inside the platform's own messages
The session brief, the sandbox link, the setup skill link, and the feedback form. Links are to the product and its documentation only, because the work needs them. Check each platform's rules on links in messages before sending.

## Rules, load-bearing
- Paid on the platform where the implementer was found.
- No request to move contact or payment off the platform.
- No partner program pitch, no partner page link, no email request inside Upwork or Fiverr.
- An honest platform review after the work.

Guardrails: implementers only, platform rules above, `rules/do-not-say.md`, `ops/QA-checklist.md` before posting.
