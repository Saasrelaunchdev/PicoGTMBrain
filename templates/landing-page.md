# Template, landing page

Audience: pick one, merchants or implementers. Never blend. Stage: name it.
Voice reference: `examples/app-store-listing.md` for merchants, `examples/cold-email-paid-session.md` for implementers.

1. **Hero.** The promise in the audience's words. Merchants: "An AI shopping assistant for Shopify that answers from your own catalog." Implementers: "Paid to test it. Credited when it works." One call to action.
2. **The problem, as they live it.** Merchants: shoppers who don't know a product's name, the same questions by hand. Implementers: clients ask about AI and there is no tested answer. No product yet.
3. **The reframe.** One line from `ops/copy-bank.md`.
4. **What it is.** One paragraph from `context/Pico_GTM_Master_Context.md` Section 5, rewritten for the audience.
5. **Pillar 1, 2, 3.** Headline, body, open proof, from the audience's value proposition file. Status in line on anything IN BUILD.
6. **How it works.** Merchants: sandbox, install, four-step checklist, read Analytics. Implementers: apply, sandbox, install on a store you work on, report, get paid.
7. **Objections.** The two from `reference/objection-handling.md` this stage raises.
8. **Proof.** Open proof only. The customer proof log is empty.
9. **What Pico does not do.** Merchants: no checkout in chat, no order answers, no revenue reporting yet. Implementers: no white label, no guaranteed leads.
10. **Pricing or terms.** Merchants: plans with units. Implementers: $150 a session, partner terms only once the partner page is live.
11. **FAQ.** Honest, including "not yet".
12. **Close.** The same call to action as the hero.

Guardrails: one audience, capability status in line, open proof only, no named competitor, `ops/QA-checklist.md` before shipping.
