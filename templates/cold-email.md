# Template, cold email to implementers

Audience: implementers serving US stores. Stage 1. Channel engine: `reference/outreach-engine.md`. Voice reference: `examples/cold-email-paid-session.md`.

## First email, under 120 words
1. **Subject.** The offer, not a tease: "Paid: $150 to test an AI shopping app on a store you work on".
2. **Line 1.** Who we are, in one line.
3. **Line 2.** The offer: "We pay you $150 to install Pico on a store you work on and tell us what works and what breaks."
4. **Scope.** Sandbox first, install on a development store or a client store with permission, the Shopper Words Test, a setup-time log, a feedback form. Up to 90 minutes.
5. **Payment.** By invoice within 7 days of the completed feedback form.
6. **CTA.** Reply to book.
7. **Footer.** Sender name, "This is an advertisement", the US postal address, a working opt-out.

## Follow-ups
- **Second email:** the sandbox, the setup skill, the technical one-pager. Or the Agency AI Shopping Readiness Scorecard for anyone not ready to book.
- **After a session:** the findings, the case study proposal with the exact wording to be published, and from week 6 the partner terms quoted exactly.

## Rules
- US recipients only in the first 90 days. Then UK companies and LLPs only. Never UK sole traders, never cold to the EU.
- Opt-outs honored within 10 business days.
- No partner rates, traffic, or income promises in the first email.
- Lists from public agency sites and directories, used within each site's terms. Reports show segments and counts only.

Guardrails: implementers only, CAN-SPAM footer on every send, `rules/do-not-say.md` implementer bans, `ops/QA-checklist.md` before sending.
