# Template, LinkedIn connection note, message, and founder post

Audience: implementers. Stages 1, 2, and 6. Sent by hand, always. Channel engine: `reference/outreach-engine.md`. Voice reference: `examples/linkedin-founder-post.md`.

## Connection note, under 300 characters
One line on the paid session: "I'm paying Shopify developers $150 to test an AI shopping assistant on a store they work on. Open to a short note?"

## First message after acceptance
1. The offer and the scope, from `templates/cold-email.md`, shortened.
2. A link to Pico's page with the session details.
3. One question that is easy to answer.

## Founder post on method
- **Opening line** that survives alone in a feed: a concrete setup fact or a shopper phrase.
- **Body,** two to four short paragraphs, one idea: install through a theme app embed, setup by coding agent, or what a zero result phrase means.
- **The turn:** the specific detail worth saving.
- **Close:** a question, or one soft CTA. Never both.

## Partnership invitation, from week 6
Session participants met by email or LinkedIn only. The partner page link and the case study invitation. Terms quoted exactly: 25% of each referred store's subscription for 24 months.

## Rules
- No automation tools, no scraping, no purchased automation. Volume is what one person can send.
- No results claimed until the proof log has an entry. No competitor named.
- No "certified", no "guaranteed leads".

Guardrails: implementers only, sent by hand, `rules/do-not-say.md`, `ops/QA-checklist.md` before sending.
