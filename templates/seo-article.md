# Template, catalog guide or comparison page

Audience: merchants. Stage 1 (guides) or stage 2 (comparison and alternative pages). Written for search engines and AI answer engines alike. Channel engine: `reference/app-store-engine.md`.

## Catalog guide (one per category: health, outdoor and sports, pets, auto parts, electronics)
1. **Title.** The question a merchant types, for example "How shoppers describe hiking boots, and how to name yours so they are found".
2. **The answer in two sentences,** at the top, quotable on its own.
3. **How shoppers describe products in this category.** Illustrative phrases, labeled as examples.
4. **Common requests and the words catalogs use instead.** A short table, sample data labeled.
5. **How to name and describe products so they can be found.** Concrete steps.
6. **Run the Shopper Words Test.** Ten phrases from customer emails and Shopify's "Searches with no results", compared with product titles.
7. **Where Pico fits.** One paragraph, then the sandbox CTA.

## Comparison or alternative page
1. **Who this page is for,** and when another option fits better, including Shopify's own tools.
2. **What each option does, its billing unit, and when it fits.** Every fact sourced, dated, page-loaded, and rechecked before each update.
3. **What Pico does not do.**
4. **The sandbox CTA.**

## Rules for both
- No industry statistic on zero results or site search. None has a primary source.
- Speak well of Shopify's own reports. Never "Shopify's search is broken".
- No comparative claim without a sourced, dated finding. Review every comparison page before it goes live.

Guardrails: merchants only, open proof, `rules/do-not-say.md` scoping section on every superlative, `ops/QA-checklist.md` before publishing.
