# Template, social post

Audience: one. Founder posts on LinkedIn reach implementers. Merchant-facing social is not a channel in this plan, so merchant posts are limited to sharing a guide.

## Structure
- **Opening line.** The hook. It must survive being read alone in a feed.
- **Body.** Two to five short paragraphs, one idea, concrete.
- **Turn.** The reframe or the specific detail that makes it worth saving.
- **Close.** A question, or one soft call to action. Never both.

## Archetypes to rotate
- Setup fact: the theme app embed, and what uninstall leaves behind.
- Coding agent setup: what the setup skill covers.
- A zero result phrase: what it can mean, a missing product or a product described in other words.
- What we are paying for: the paid session, its scope and fee.
- A finding from the session digest, only with permission and only as method.

## Platform notes
- LinkedIn: no external link in the first line, a short paragraph per thought, native video for the setup walkthrough.

Guardrails: one audience, no fabricated metrics or testimonials, open proof only, no em dashes, `ops/QA-checklist.md` before posting.
