# Template, case study

Audience: one per case study, merchant-facing or implementer-facing. Stage 6. Nothing here ships until the proof log has an entry for it.

## Gate, all four or nothing
1. The proof point, stated.
2. Its exact permitted wording.
3. Its source: the store's Analytics export, the session log, the feedback form.
4. Recorded permission from everyone it names: the merchant, and the implementer if credited.

Add the entry to the proof log in `ops/copy-bank.md` through an AMEND, then write.

## Shape
1. **Headline.** What changed, in the permitted wording only.
2. **The store.** Category, catalog size, platform plan, as permitted. Named only with permission.
3. **The starting point.** What shoppers asked that found nothing, in the store's own phrases.
4. **What was done.** The catalog or description fixes, the setup, and who did it. Credit the implementer when permitted.
5. **What Pico measured.** Zero result rate on requests, clicks, add to cart, against the store's own baseline. No revenue figure until attribution exists.
6. **In their words.** A quote only if it is in the permission form.
7. **CTA.** Merchants: the sandbox. Implementers: a paid session.

## Never
A composite store, an estimate presented as a result, a number outside the permission, or a quote that was paraphrased.

Guardrails: proof log entry first, one audience, `rules/do-not-say.md`, `ops/QA-checklist.md` before publishing.
