# Glossary, canonical vocabulary

Used in the product, the dashboard, the docs, and every asset. Mirror it exactly.
last_reviewed: 2026-11-04

## Names

| Term | Rule |
|---|---|
| Pico | The product and brand in prose. Capital P, no "PICO", no "Pico AI" as a bare name |
| PicoFinds | The company and project name. Domain: picofinds.com. Contact: us@picofinds.com |
| Pico AI Shopping Assistant | The Shopify App Store listing name, in full on the listing and in App Store copy. It separates Pico from "Piko ‑ Pickup Reminder" |
| Pico Assistant | The theme app embed's name, exactly as it appears in the Shopify theme editor |
| AI shopping assistant | The category. Lowercase in prose |

## People

| Term | Rule |
|---|---|
| merchant | The store that installs and pays. Not "user" in copy, not "client" unless an implementer is speaking |
| shopper | The person asking in the widget. Never "customer" when the merchant could misread it as their own buyer list |
| implementer | Agencies of every kind, Shopify developers, theme developers, and freelancers. "Agency" alone excludes freelancers |
| partner, lead partner | An implementer accepted into the program, and one with 5 active paying stores. Only after the partner page is live in week 6 |
| founding cohort | The first 20 US merchant stores. Never "beta" or "early access program" |

## Product

| Term | Rule |
|---|---|
| conversation | The billing unit: a chat thread with at least one AI reply. Messages from the same shopper on the same device within 24 hours count once. Fair use: 50 AI replies |
| synced catalog | The source of every product card. Always "synced", because stock is as of the last catalog sync |
| product card | Image, name, price, stock label, and a View link |
| Analytics page, Overview, Catalog, Integrations, Assistant, Plan & Usage, Settings | The seven dashboard pages, capitalized as named |
| discovery funnel | Request to result, click, and add to cart, with step conversion |
| zero result phrases, weak click phrases | Requests to the assistant that returned no products, or products that drew few clicks |
| sandbox store | The sample outdoor gear catalog on the real API. Never "demo store" in copy |
| setup skill | The downloadable file a coding agent follows. Never "AI installer" |
| theme app embed | How Pico reaches the storefront. Never "plugin" or "script injection" for Shopify |
| quota meter | The usage bar on Plan & Usage |

## Programs and magnets

| Term | Rule |
|---|---|
| Shopper Words Test | The merchant demand driver. Capitalized, never "audit" |
| Paid Usability Session | The implementer demand driver: $150, up to 90 minutes |
| Client Store Findability Audit | The service an implementer can sell from the session's findings |
| Shopify Store Findability Scorecard, Agency AI Shopping Readiness Scorecard | The two ScoreApp lead magnets, named in full |
| open proof | A claim the reader can check now, labelled as checkable. Never presented as a customer result |
| proof log | The closed list of permissioned customer proof. Empty on 2026-11-04 |
| managed share, deal registration | The 10% share on stores a partner manages but did not refer, and the 90-day claim on a client store's myshopify domain |

## Banned near-synonyms

| Do not use | Why, and what to use |
|---|---|
| chatbot | Pico positions against "another AI chatbot". Use AI shopping assistant |
| semantic search, AI search engine | There is no vector search. Describe the matching |
| live stock, real-time inventory | Stock is as of the last catalog sync |
| checkout in chat | Pico adds to the store's own cart. Checkout stays in the store |
| Pico Partner Program, certified partner | No program name before week 6, and no certification exists |

## Distinctions that must never blur

- **Conversation vs AI reply.** The quota counts conversations. Zipchat bills per AI reply. Mixing them misstates every price comparison.
- **Search-bar zero results vs zero result phrases.** Shopify's Search & Discovery already reports search-bar terms with no results. Pico's report covers requests made to the assistant. Every insight claim depends on this line.
- **Referred vs managed store.** 25% or 30% for referred stores, 10% for managed ones, one share per store.
- **Add to cart vs checkout.** Pico ends at the store's own cart.

The distinction that matters most: Pico reports what shoppers asked the assistant, not what they typed in the search bar.
