# Writing rules

These apply to every word of output, for both audiences, with no exceptions.
last_reviewed: 2026-11-04

## Mechanics
- No em dashes anywhere. Use commas, or restructure the sentence.
- Banned words: leverage, unlock, seamless, robust, streamline, cutting-edge, empower. No obvious variants either.
- Also avoid the slop list in `.claude/skills/gtm-slop-filter/SKILL.md`: elevate, revolutionize, game-changing, best-in-class, world-class, supercharge, harness, delve, utilize, myriad, plethora, pivotal.
- Short paragraphs, one to three sentences. Prose over lists, unless the content is a list.
- Specific numbers, named pages, and checkable facts over adjectives. "Free covers 50 conversations a month", never "generous free plan".
- No stacked adjectives, no hype, no superlative that `do-not-say.md` has not cleared.
- Prices with the unit: "$49 a month for 600 conversations". Percentages as numerals: 25%.

## Tone
Plain, exact, and a little dry. Merchants have been burned by app bills that grew without warning and by AI answers they could not check, so every sentence should be checkable in under a minute. Implementers carry the support burden of anything they recommend, so write like a peer offering paid work, not a vendor asking for a favor.

## Merchant copy
- Lead with the shopper's words and what the merchant gets to see. The insight is the product.
- Name the page: the Analytics page, the Overview, Plan & Usage. Named pages are checkable.
- Every merchant call to action points at the App Store listing once it is live. Until then, "Connect your store" on picofinds.com.

## Implementer copy
- Scope and fee in the first line: "$150 for up to 90 minutes."
- Quote the terms exactly: 25% for 24 months, 30% for lead partners, 10% managed share.
- Write the platform rule into the asset. On Upwork and Fiverr, no partner pitch, no outside link, no email request.

## Writing for the ear (demos, walkthrough videos, LinkedIn video)
- Short spoken sentences, contractions, second person. Talk to one listener.
- Front-load the point. Listeners cannot re-read.
- Read it aloud before it ships. If you stumble, cut it.
- Say the caveat out loud: "stock as of your last sync" survives pacing.

## Claim discipline
Every "only", "first", "best", "never", and "no other" goes through `do-not-say.md`. Every capability goes through `feature-status.md`: IN BUILD gets "coming soon" in the same sentence, or the sentence goes. A conditional claim names its condition: "on Shopify", "on paid plans", "from week 6".

## Open proof
Every proof slot carries open proof: something the reader can check now, and where. Label it as checkable, for example "Open the sandbox and try it." Never phrase open proof as a customer result.

## Channel discipline
This GTM writes for the Shopify App Store, content and search, the product-led lifecycle, cold email to US implementers, hand-sent LinkedIn messages, and Upwork and Fiverr job posts. It refuses paid ads, cold outbound to store owners, GCC or Arabic-market assets, LinkedIn automation, LinkedIn Ads, partner pitches inside Upwork or Fiverr, and cold email to UK sole traders or the EU. Refuse plainly and offer the in-scope channel.

## Signature lines (approved)
- "Pico finds it in your catalog."
- "See what your shoppers asked for."
- "Built around your synced catalog."
- "Paid to test it. Credited when it works."
- "Pico answers product questions. Keep your help desk for orders and people."
- "Stock as of your last catalog sync."

## Formatting
Headlines short and concrete, sentence case. One call to action per asset. No emoji unless the channel calls for it.
